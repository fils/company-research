# Fleetzero – Raw Web Extract
**Source:** https://www.fleetzero.com/

## Overview
Fleetzero is a marine technology company that designs, manufactures, and sells ultra energy-dense modular marine battery systems (Leviathan) and hybrid/electric propulsion systems for new and existing commercial vessels. The company is pioneering modular hull construction to lower shipbuilding costs and increase shipyard output.

## Products & Technology
- **Leviathan Modular Energy Storage System**: The safest and most efficient marine battery system in the world. Scalable from modules to megawatts. Double the power at half the price compared to other marine solutions. 3.8 MWh of energy in 25% of the size of a standard container footprint.
- **Marine Battery Container**: 3.8 MWh energy in a compact footprint. Capable of moving cargo across continents (Pacific Ocean and back on a single charge).
- **Battery Barge**: 55 MWh energy storage, mobile to fit fleet needs, expandable capacity. Charges multiple vessels.
- **Modular Propulsion**: Electric propulsion system enabling modular ship construction. Payback period under 6 years, many positive within 2 years.
- **Modular Hull Construction**: Pioneering modular hull construction to lower costs and increase shipyard output.

## Business Model
- **Direct sales**: Custom solutions for vessel electrification
- **End-to-end integration**: Concept to commissioning (white glove service)
- **Price parity with fossil fuels**: No green premium; nearly every project results in payback under 6 years
- **Service**: Custom hybridized solutions for extending vessel life
