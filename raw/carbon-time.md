## Summary
Carbon Time is a European pioneer in Ocean Alkalinity Enhancement (OAE) using multiple approaches (natural rock weathering, electrochemical, biological, coastal preservation). Permanent CO₂ storage as dissolved inorganic carbon (DIC) for 20,000+ years. Team for the Planet's first innovation beneficiary.

## Business Model
- Carbon removal credits via ocean alkalinity enhancement
- B2B partnerships with coastal/marine infrastructure (desalination plants, aquaculture, coastal sites)
- Science-backed MRV with public scientists
- Synergistic use of existing industrial infrastructure

## Technology
- Four OAE methods: natural rock weathering (olivine), electrochemical approaches, biological (shellfish farming integration), coastal preservation (mangroves/seagrass/salt marshes)
- Compatible with desalination plants, oyster farms, artificial islands, post-dredging sites
- Megatons CO₂ scalable theoretically
- Zero risk of reversibility — deep layer storage
