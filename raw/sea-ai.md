### Tagline
Now you see — Machine Vision for Safety at Sea

### Pitch
SEA.AI alerts crews early and reliably about objects on the surface of the water. Using latest camera technology in combination with artificial intelligence, SEA.AI detects and classifies objects, including those that escape conventional systems such as Radar or AIS: unsignalled craft, floating obstacles, buoys, inflatables, kayaks and persons over board.

## Spotlight Use Case: Counter-USV Detection

"Radar sees a contact. AIS shows a name, if the vessel chooses to broadcast one. When a threat USV does neither, **optical AI is the only sensor layer that still sees it.**"

SEA.AI equips naval forces and coast guards with **AI-powered visual intelligence** that detects, classifies, and tracks non-cooperative surface contacts in real time, day and night.
