## Overview
Calcarea decarbonizes shipping through scalable carbon capture and storage (CCS) solutions that are safe, permanent, and economical. Based on over a decade of research at Caltech and USC, the company has developed an "Accelerated Weathering of Limestone" reactor for marine on-board carbon capture.

## Technology
- Accelerated Weathering of Limestone (AWL) reactor for ship-board CO2 capture
- Converts captured CO2 into oceanic bicarbonate (stable, secure, environmentally benign)
- Smaller, more efficient, lower cost than current marine CCS alternatives
- Eliminates dependency on port infrastructure for handling pure CO2
- Can capture from point sources and safely store atmospheric carbon

## Business Model
