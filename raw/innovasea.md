## Summary
Innovasea provides sustainable aquaculture technology: submersible net pens, environmental monitoring, underwater cameras, fish tracking (NexTrak acoustic telemetry), and AI-powered fish counting (HydroAI). Global presence with offices in Chile, Canada, and US.

## Business Model
- B2B aquaculture technology and equipment sales
- Fish tracking systems (acoustic tags, receivers, NexTrak)
- Environmental monitoring solutions
- Ongoing customer support and SaaS for data analytics
- Products for both open ocean and land-based aquaculture

## Technology
- Submersible Net Pens (patented — fully submerged operation for extended periods)
- NexTrak Fish Tracking (precision acoustic telemetry)
- HydroAI (AI-powered fish counting/weight estimation)
- Environmental sensors (aquaMeasure)
- Underwater camera systems
- Cloud-based data communications

## Funding & Backers
