"As environmental pressures rise, aquaculture faces growing challenges: oxygen depletion, harmful algal blooms, fluctuating feed intake, and increased fish stress. Making the right decisions at the right time is key, not only for performance, but for long-term sustainability.

## Business Model
- **SaaS subscription** for water quality monitoring/forecasting (AquaCHECK/AquaFORECAST/AquaLYTICS products)
- **Data integration**: pulls from real-time sensors, open/satellite/sampling data to produce forecasts and analytics
- **Multi-country deployment** (10+ countries) — Chilean salmon (PREDIMAR), Norwegian expansion (Alexia Mouton country lead), other aquaculture regions
- **Domain expertise as moat**: 20+ experts (data scientists, oceanographers, marine biologists, fish health biologists) — biology+AI combination as differentiated approach vs pure-software competitors
