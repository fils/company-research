**Tagline:** "Raman Spectroscopy – Made Accessible"

**Founder/CEO:** Nili Persits

## Core Features
- **Accurate** – detects nutrients, metabolic markers, organic & non-organic waste products
- **Sustainable** – reagent-free and completely non-destructive optical sensors
- **Real-Time** – continuous or timed sensing with no delay
