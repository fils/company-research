# Aquaticode – Raw Web Extract

**Source:** https://aquaticode.com/

## Company Overview
Aquaticode builds AI-powered technology for aquaculture, specifically targeting the salmon farming industry. The company uses machine learning and computer vision to identify performance traits in farmed fish, enabling farmers to boost production efficiency.

**Location:** Norway (part of NCE Seafood Innovation Cluster)
**CEO:** Stian Rognlid
**Founded by:** Nacre Capital (venture builder)

## Products

### SORTpro
- Automatically sorts salmon by gender and other performance traits at high speed
- 10,000 juvenile salmon per hour
- >95% accuracy for salmon weighing 20-100 grams
- Gender-separated salmon is >20% more efficient
- Non-invasive identification using computer vision

### SORTvax
- Turns line-speed vaccination into a decision point
- Groups fish for robustness, removes unviable fish, boosts profitability

### Bass/Bream Solutions
- Launching solutions for bass/bream farmers in 2026

## Technology
- Machine vision trained on >3 million images and videos
- >15 industrial research partnerships
- Predicts fish and shrimp phenotypes through machine vision
- Researching interaction between genotype (DNA) and phenotype (visual expression)
- Capable of detecting, identifying and predicting multiple performance traits
- Patented technology (approved patents for SORTpro)
