## Overview
Boxfish Robotics (Auckland, NZ) designs and manufactures hovering AUVs, expedition ROVs, and resident autonomous underwater vehicles (ARV-i) for marine science, environmental monitoring, coral reef/benthic assessment, infrastructure inspection, aquaculture, and underwater filming. 11-50 employees. NVIDIA Jetson AI, ROS2, 6DOF thrusters for precision. Active at ICRS 2026 demos. Reliable for research and conservation. Customers include University of Auckland, Leaf Global Environmental Services, Inspired Planet Productions.

- Hardware sales (AUV, ROV, ARV) + accessories (photogrammetry, lighting)
- Solutions for marine research, environmental monitoring, aquaculture resident ops, cinematography
- Training, support, case studies-driven sales
- Tetherless autonomy for persistent underwater operations without human presence

## Funding / Stage

## Key Technology

- ARV-i: resident tetherless AUV for long-term deployments (aquaculture, infrastructure)
