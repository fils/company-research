# FishLAT (Blue Latitudes) – Raw Web Extract
**Source:** https://www.fishlat.com/

## Company Overview
FishLAT is a rapid assessment tool that uses machine learning to predict how the removal, reefing, or installation of offshore infrastructure will impact the environment and fisheries. Provides data transparency to offshore development.

## Core Product / Services
- **Remove it** – FishLAT provides valuable data for decommissioning decision making, giving regulators and energy industry information on how removal of a structure may impact fisheries
- **Plan it** – Provides predictive data for future development offshore, giving regulators and energy industry the information on how the installation of a structure may impact fisheries
- **Reef it** – Provides information for decisions to reef an offshore structure, giving regulators, energy industry, and other fisheries stakeholders information on how repurposing a structure as an artificial reef may impact fisheries

## Value Proposition
- Rapid, data-rich, cost-effective
- Has successfully supported the permitting of reefing sites
- Supports alignment of marine development and fisheries sectors
