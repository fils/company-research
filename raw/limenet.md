# Limenet – Raw Web Extract
**Source:** https://limenet.tech/
**Sector:** Ocean Carbon Sequestration
**Last Updated:** 2026-05-15

## Summary
Limenet produces carbon-free lime enabling permanent CO₂ removal and ocean storage as an aqueous solution of calcium bicarbonate with the same pH as seawater. Patented technology with 4 patent families. ISO 14064-2 certified MRV.

## Business Model
- B2B carbon removal credits via bicarbonate storage in oceans
- Stripe, Shopify, KlimaDAO, Frontier Climate, Google as offtakers
- Selling CDR credits to corporate buyers

## Technology
- Calcium carbonate + seawater + renewable energy → aqueous calcium bicarbonates
- Naturally accelerates carbon cycle; counters ocean acidification
- 10,000-100,000 year CO₂ permanence
- Current capacity: 800 tons CO₂/year (2024 facility in Augusta, Sicily)
- Next plant (2026-27): 1,500 tons decarbonized lime/year

## Funding & Backers
- Moonstone, Axel Carbon, Leone La Ferla S.p.A., CDP, EU/Next Generation EU, Fassa Bortolo, Aither

## Marine Data & Measurement Needs
- ISO 14064-2 MRV for carbon credit verification
- Requires in-situ seawater chemistry monitoring (pH, alkalinity, DIC, calcium concentration)
- Ocean alkalinity enhancement verification requires continuous carbonate chemistry monitoring at outfall
- Ecosystem impact monitoring around deployment sites (Sicily, Italy)
- Needs real-time pH and alkalinity sensors for process control and regulatory compliance
