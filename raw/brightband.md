**Source:** https://www.brightband.com/

## Overview
Brightband is building an end-to-end AI Earth System for probabilistic weather and climate forecasting. Founded by ex-Google X executive Julian Green (CEO), with Co-Founders Daniel Rothenberg (Head of Data & Weather, MIT atmospheric scientist, former Chief Scientist at Tomorrow.io) and Ryan Keisler (Chief Scientist, former Staff Data Scientist at KoBold Metals, AI weather pioneer). The company open-sources benchmark datasets, models, and metrics to establish a common task for global weather forecasting.
