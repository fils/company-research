## Platform: Three Core Layers

### 1. Data Fabric – Unify & Govern Every Source
- Streaming + historical ingestion with automated metadata capture
- Normalization, QC, and retention policies built for compliance
- Secure workspaces and APIs for every partner

### 2. AI-Ready – Make Data Analytics-Ready Fast
- YSOK AI sits on top of curated datasets
- Scenario engines for risk, hydrokinetic yield, and resilience planning
- ML layers flag anomalies, events, and opportunities
- Explainable outputs for underwriting, permitting, and policy

### 3. Insights – Deliver Decisions Anywhere
- Role-based briefs for scientists, mariners, civic leaders, and residents
- API + export options feed underwriting, SCADA, and grant systems
- Audit trails for sensor-to-outcome traceability
