## Overview
Gigablue is a marine carbon dioxide removal (mCDR) company pioneering Microalgae Carbon Fixation and Sinking (MCFS). Uses microalgae + proprietary substrates deployed in deep ocean (>1000m) for durable sequestration. AI-driven oceanographic intelligence platform optimizes deployment zones, substrate composition, and timing using satellite, in-situ sensors, robots, and historical data. Focus on lowest-cost scalable durable CDR with high-integrity credits.

## Business Model
- Commercial sale of permanent carbon removal credits to corporations/governments (e.g., SkiesFifty agreement for large volumes).
- Nature-based MCFS methodology aligned with Puro.Earth MCFS v1 (2025).
- Low cost-barrier scaling via ocean's natural carbon cycle acceleration.
- Partners: Carbon Plant Exchange, SkiesFifty, RAIN Foundation, WOC.

## Funding / Investors / Milestones
- Signed major CDR credit agreement with SkiesFifty (largest ocean carbon removal deal announced).
- Research trials (2024-2025): Mediterranean, New Zealand EEZ (Great South Basin), with EPA NZ permitting.
- Partners with Earth Sciences New Zealand (NIWA), Cawthron Institute for monitoring/analysis.
- Emphasis on transparent MMRV (Monitoring, Measurement, Reporting, Verification) including custom ROVs for deep-ocean.

## Key Technology
- Microalgae Carbon Fixation & Sinking (MCFS): Deploy substrate that stimulates phytoplankton bloom → carbon fixation → sinking to deep seafloor (>1000m).
- AI platform: Predicts optimal sites, particle recipes per season/location.
- Extensive deep-sea monitoring: ROVs, sensors, dispersion modeling, oxygen drawdown models.
- Regulatory: EPA NZ permitted activities, London Convention/Protocol compliant; small-scale trials with negligible nutrient impact (<1.7% macronutrient change).

## Marine Data Needs
### Primary Data Types
- Real-time and historical oceanographic sensor data (temperature, salinity, dissolved oxygen, currents, nutrients).
- Satellite-derived productivity, chlorophyll-a, bathymetry.
- In-situ benthic and water-column samples for MMRV.
- Dispersion/fate modeling for substrate plumes.

### Specific Measurements / Parameters
- Carbonate chemistry parameters (pH, DIC, alkalinity) for baseline and impact.
- Phytoplankton productivity, biomass stocks.
- Seafloor deposition density and oxygen consumption.
- Nutrient levels (N, P, Si) to avoid depletion.
- Benthic community metrics and biodiversity impacts.

### Observation Platforms / Programs
- Autonomous underwater vehicles / ROVs (custom-built deep-ocean).
- Fixed moorings and sensor networks in AOI (Great South Basin).
- Satellite validation campaigns.
- Extensive pre/post-trial sampling campaigns.

### Known Data Gaps / Pain Points
- Requires high-resolution spatial and temporal data for optimal site selection and risk assessment of local nutrient depletion or oxygen drawdown.
- Transparent MMRV demands continuous deep-ocean telemetry which is expensive and logistically challenging.
- Regulatory compliance needs detailed baseline ecosystem surveys.
