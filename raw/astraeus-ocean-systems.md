# Astraeus Ocean Systems – Raw Web Extract
**Source:** https://www.astraeusocean.com/

## Overview
Astraeus Ocean Systems (Bellingham, WA) provides direct access to a smart network of autonomous vessels for rapid, cost-effective ocean data delivery. Their primary product is "The Mariculture Almanac" — a biological crop modeling, in-water monitoring, and farm task management system for shellfish and seaweed operations.

## Funding & Programs
- VentureWell Ocean Enterprise Accelerator Stage 2 (Spring 2026) – $50K non-dilutive TDC award
- Bellingham Angel Investors pitch competition winner (2024)
- Described as "Uber for ocean intelligence" by GeekWire

## Products
1. **Watchline™ Hardware** – Deployed sensor hardware measuring: temperature, salinity, dissolved oxygen, and proprietary optical factors. GPS monitoring for offshore infrastructure. Dockside variant for shore-based operations.
2. **The Almanac Platform** – Tracks stock, infrastructure, and water conditions across all sites. Forecasts crop development. Simulates decisions (harvest timing, relay, thinning). Mobile-first, works offline.
3. **Digital Oyster™ & Digital Kelp™** – Proprietary biological state models of shellfish and seaweed physiology. Continuously updated from seeding through harvest. Condition, stress, growth rate, market readiness.
4. **Astraeus Mission Planner** – Autonomous vessel fleet coordination platform.

## Key Measurements
- Temperature, salinity, dissolved oxygen (DO)
- Proprietary optical factors feeding crop models
- Chlorophyll-a (Chl-a), Na⁺, NO₃⁻, Ca²⁺
- GPS position monitoring for infrastructure
- Water quality parameters for regulatory compliance

## Business Model
Subscription-based: one fee covers Watchline hardware, crop model access, Almanac platform, and support. Annual subscription per farm/site.

## Data Needs
- High-resolution in-situ water quality data at farm lease sites
- Continuous environmental monitoring (temperature, salinity, DO, optical)
- Crop physiological model validation data
- Metocean forecasts for operational planning
- Regulatory compliance documentation
