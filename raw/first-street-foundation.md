# First Street Foundation
**Source:** https://firststreet.org/
**Sector:** Climate Risk
**Last Updated:** 2026-05-02

## Business Model
"The Standard for Climate Risk Financial Modeling" – provides physics-based climate risk data, analytics and financial modeling for real estate, finance, insurance, government. Enterprise SaaS + data licensing. Revenue from subscriptions, reports, API access for asset/portfolio due diligence, stress testing, regulatory compliance.

## Funding & Backers
Non-profit/impact focused with major adoption. Trusted by Fannie Mae, Blackstone, Allstate, EPA, NOAA, Zillow. Recognized by Fast Company 2026 Most Innovative.

## Key Tech
Purpose-built deterministic physics models, building-specific archetypes, multi-peril (flood, wildfire, wind, etc.), global coverage, validated against events.

## Marine Data Needs
Moderate to high: Coastal/flood risk modeling requires detailed bathymetry, sea-level rise projections, storm surge data, shoreline change. Collaborates with NOAA; strong demand for oceanographic inputs in coastal asset risk assessment.

## Highlights
Property-level risk scores freely available for wildfire in some cases. Focus on integrating physical climate risk into financial decisions (mortgage default correlation with flood risk).
