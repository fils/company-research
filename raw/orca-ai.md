### Overview
Orca AI provides AI-powered computer vision and situational awareness for maritime navigation safety and collision avoidance. Founded 2018 by Yarden Gross (CEO) and Dor Raviv (CTO), based in Tel Aviv, Israel.

### Products
- **SeaPod** — Marine situational awareness system for the bridge; automated lookout with target detection, alerts, and prioritization even in near-zero visibility and congested waters
- **FleetView** — Fleet-level visibility into maritime safety & operations; SMS compliance monitoring, sustainability tracking, profitability analysis
- **Co-Captain** — Autonomous navigation assistant ("Waze of the sea"); real-time route recommendations
- **MasterView** — Decision support for ship masters
- **Perception for autonomous vessels** — AI perception layer for autonomous shipping

### Key Metrics
- 1,500+ vessels booked with the platform
- 120M+ nautical miles of data collected
- World's largest marine visual dataset (80M+ nautical miles)
- 58% increase in SMS navigation policy compliance across 107-vessel fleet
- $100K fuel saving per vessel per year (Seaspan case study)
- 74% reduction in close encounter events (Maran Tankers case study)
- 54% reduction in close encounter events (2024 analysis)

### Business Model
SaaS platform with hardware (SeaPod sensor unit) installed on vessels; subscription for FleetView office-side analytics. Dual-revenue: per-vessel hardware + recurring software. Expanding into defense & government sectors.

### Industries
Commercial shipping, defense & government

### Data & Measurement Needs
- **Primary data types**: Real-time visual/sensor streams from bridge-mounted cameras (day/night/thermal), AIS data, vessel telemetry, GPS positioning
- **Key measurements/parameters**: Target distance (CPA/TCPA), vessel heading, speed, bearing, visibility conditions, sea state, traffic density
- **Observation platforms/programs**: Ship-mounted optical + thermal sensors, fleet-wide data collection network (1,500+ vessels), satellite connectivity (Starlink-enabled)
- **Known data gaps**: Limited coverage in polar regions, deep ocean transit zones; relies on vessel density for network effect
- **Interest in external data services**: Satellite AIS augmentation, weather routing data integration, ocean current data for voyage optimization
