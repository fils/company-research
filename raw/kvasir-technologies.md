# Kvasir Technologies – Raw Web Extract
**Source:** https://kvasirtechnologies.com/

## Overview
Kvasir Technologies is a Danish cleantech company that has invented a novel technology for converting plant biomass (lignocellulosic agricultural and forestry waste) into a CO2-neutral drop-in biofuel that serves as a 1:1 substitute for fossil marine fuel. The technology requires no engine modifications or infrastructure changes — "Change the fuel, not the engine."

## Technology
- **Simple one-step process**: Pressurized "cooking" of biomass in alcohol — best described as pressurized liquefaction
- **>96% GHG emission reduction** vs fossil marine fuel — close to fully carbon-neutral
- **100% sustainable**: Relies on non-edible plant material only (lignocellulose from agricultural and forestry waste — abundantly available)
- **Scalable**: Agricultural and forestry waste products are abundantly available
- **Drop-in replacement**: No switching costs or added storage cost; bolts onto the current marine fuel supply chain; handles both existing fossil fuels and green biofuel interchangeably

## Markets
1. **Marine Fuel** (primary, low-hanging fruit): Bio-oil can be used "as is" with no post-processing requirements. Enables existing fleet to meet IMO 2030 (40% reduction) and 2050 (50% reduction) targets.
2. **Bio Crude Oil** (largest volume): Co-feeding bio-oil into existing refineries for greener refinery outputs
3. **Aviation Fuel** (largest single market impact): Selective conversion to climate-neutral jet fuel
4. **Value Added Chemicals** (highest value): High-value aromatic chemicals and bio-plastics from selective catalytic conversion

## Business Model
- **Fuel production and licensing**: Produces drop-in biofuel for marine fuel supply chain
- **No infrastructure barrier**: Solution integrates into existing fuel supply chain without modification
- **Cost-competitive**: CO2-neutral fuel at cost parity with fossil fuels
- **Seamless transition**: Allows full flexibility between fossil and biofuel — enables incremental decarbonization
