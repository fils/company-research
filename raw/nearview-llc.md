# Nearview LLC – Raw Web Extract
**Source:** https://venturewell.org/spring-2026-stage-1-ocean-enterprise-accelerator-cohort/

## Business Model
Nearview LLC provides multi-object-detection AI that identifies lobster buoys, boats, and aquaculture gear from aerial/satellite imagery to support marine-user deconfliction.

### Key Technology
- Multi-object AI detection from aerial imagery
- Multi-object AI detection from satellite imagery
- Lobster buoy detection
- Boat detection
- Aquaculture gear detection
- Marine user deconfliction
