# AquaExchange – Raw Web Extract
**Source:** https://aquaexchange.com/

## Overview
Aqua Exchange is transforming aquaculture with innovative IoT technology and data-driven insights. The company provides a comprehensive, affordable full-stack platform for shrimp farm management — combining farm automation, AI analytics, marketplace, embedded finance, and insurance into one smart ecosystem. Founded 2020 in Vijayawada, India.

## Scale
- **20,000 devices deployed**
- **80,000 acres monitored**
- **4 countries**
- Serving the shrimp industry community for 5+ years

## Products — One Smart Ecosystem
### Farm Monitoring Products
- **CheckTray**: Smart check tray system for monitoring feed consumption and shrimp behavior patterns
- **WaterMon**: GPS-enabled smart handheld device for accurate, traceable, real-time water quality monitoring across ponds
- **Patholense**: Advanced pathogen detection system using AI-powered microscopy for early disease identification
- **PumpMon**: Monitor pump performance and water flow in real time to prevent failures and improve efficiency

### Farm Automation Products
- **PowerMon**: Real-time power monitoring with instant fault alerts — never lose a crop to power failure
- **PowerMon & APFC**: Intelligent monitoring and automatic power factor correction to cut power penalties and optimize efficiency
- **AquaBot**: Automate feeding across the entire pond with precision movement, controlled dosage, and full app control
- **Static Feeder 100 Kgs / 200 Kgs**: App-controlled feeding with 100kg or 200kg hopper for consistent daily performance
- **Feed Mixer**: Uniform, high-efficiency feed blending for additives and probiotics
- **Starter**: Smart aerator controller with remote management and motor protection
- **Smart Scale**: Real-time weight validation synced directly to feeder and app
- **FeedMon**: Intelligent listening technology that feeds only when shrimp demand it — minimizes waste and maximizes FCR
- **ProBrew Master**: Brew probiotics at the perfect temperature for consistent potency
- **Shrimp Counter**: AI-powered smartphone imaging for counting PL and shrimp in seconds

### Software
- **NextFarm App**: Comprehensive farm management application for data-driven decision making and operational efficiency
