**Source:** https://www.sofarocean.com/

## Company Overview
Sofar Ocean operates the largest privately-owned ocean sensor network in the world. The company captures real-time ocean data to reduce fuel costs, lower emissions, detect threats, and protect maritime assets at a global scale. San Francisco HQ.

## Scale of Operations
- **1.5M** real-time observations per day
- **2,500+** Spotters deployed globally
- **25M+** hours of ocean observations

## Products

### Spotter Platform
Global network of solar-powered sensing platforms measuring waves, weather, and subsurface data in real time. Built for governments, researchers, and defense.

- **Surface sensing**: Real-time waves, wind, and weather
- **Subsurface sensing**: Sound, currents, temperature, water quality, and water level
- **Spotter Scout**: Mobile autonomous — survey, transit, and station-keeping capabilities

### Wayfinder
Voyage optimization software recommending best route, speed, and schedule for each voyage. Built for shipping companies, commercial operators, charterers, and vessel performance managers.

- **4–8%** average fuel reduction
- Daily routing & RPM guidance
- Real-time storm warnings and seakeeping alerts
- One shared view across ship and shoreside

### Marine Weather
Real data, real models, better forecasts. From their own sensor network, running intelligence models from global weather to hyperlocal forecasts.
- **50%** more accurate than traditional models
