## Summary
ReelData AI provides an AI-driven suite for land-based aquaculture (RAS). Focuses on smolt, post-smolt, and on-growing operations. AI solutions for smart feeding (ReelAppetite), biomass monitoring (ReelWeight), camera systems (ReelVision), stress detection (ReelStress), and health monitoring (ReelHealth).

## Business Model
- SaaS/software licensing to aquaculture operations
- AI camera hardware (ReelVision) — custom-built for aquaculture
- Per-farm pricing model
- B2B sales to land-based RAS operators worldwide

## Technology
- ReelAppetite: automated feeding based on real-time appetite detection
- ReelWeight: non-intrusive autonomous biomass estimation
- ReelVision: AI cameras for fish behavior and feeding monitoring
- ReelStress: real-time stress detection integrating feeding patterns and water quality
- ReelHealth: early disease and maturation detection via imaging
