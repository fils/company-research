# Omission Inc – Raw Web Extract
**Source:** https://www.omissioninc.com/

## Business Model
Omission Inc manufactures unmanned systems (USVs, UGVs) and provides ocean intelligence as a service. They deploy autonomous assets to collect marine data and provide raw, processed, or analyzed datasets directly to secure dashboards. "Data as a Service" — clients don't need to invest in hardware fleets.

### Hardware Platforms
- **Unmanned Surface Vessels (USVs)**: Precision-engineered for sustained maritime operations. Support sensing, survey, security with mission-specific payloads.
- **Unmanned Ground Vehicles (UGVs)**: Built for extreme environments — inspection, sensing, logistics.
- **Custom Engineering & Manufacturing**: In-house multi-axis CNC manufacturing, electrical systems, mechanical design, pre-preg composites.

### Ocean Intelligence / Data Services
Clients receive data without investing in hardware fleets. Data types include:
- Bathymetry: High-res seabed mapping
- Water Quality: pH, salinity, turbidity
- Surveillance: EO/IR and multispectral
- Meteorological: Wind, wave, current data
