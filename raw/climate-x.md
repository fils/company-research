## Overview
Climate X provides physical climate risk data and analytics for financial institutions, real estate, and asset managers. Converts climate signals into financial loss metrics and adaptation planning tools.

## Core Products
- **Spectra**: Physical climate risk analytics at asset/portfolio level
- **Adapt**: Climate adaptation & resilience software, models CapEx/ROI for resilience measures (aligned with ISO, EU Taxonomy)
- **Carta**: Global corporate asset mapping (18.7M companies), risk & opportunity discovery

## Business Model
- Software-as-a-Service platform sold to banks, insurers, real estate funds, consultancies
- Enterprise subscription for Spectra + Adapt
- Trusted by institutions managing >$13.5 trillion AUM
- Delivers regulatory compliance support (ECB, IFRS S2, EU Taxonomy, SSB5/25)

## Key Features
- Quantifies physical climate risk into financial loss metrics
- Scenario analysis and portfolio stress testing
- Adaptation ROI modeling
- Automated reporting aligned with latest regulations
- Global coverage with asset-level precision

## Clients & Recognition
Used by Arrow Global, Equans, JLL, Deloitte, Legal & General, Carlyle, European Investment Bank, Virgin Money, Grosvenor, Triodos Bank. Strong claims for breadth/depth in risk insights from top banks and the Bank of England’s Residential Property Forum chair.

## Marine / Ocean Relevance
While primarily land and asset-focused, many clients include ports, coastal real estate, marine infrastructure, shipping companies. Physical risk data is highly applicable to offshore energy and coastal climate adaptation projects.
