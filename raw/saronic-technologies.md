**Source:** https://www.saronic.com/

## Company Overview
Saronic Technologies designs and manufactures autonomous surface vessels (ASVs) for defense and maritime autonomy. Founded in 2023, based in Austin, Texas. Recognized in a16z American Dynamism 50: AI Edition.

## Vessel Product Line
- **Spyglass**: 6-foot ASV, entry-level reconnaissance
- **Corsair**: 24' ASV, range 1,000+ nm, top speed 35+ kts, payload 1,000 lbs — multi-mission capabilities at scale
- **Mirage**: 52' ASV, range 2,500+ nm, top speed 35+ kts, payload 3,500 lbs — force multiplier for manned/unmanned teams
- **Marauder**: 180' ASV, range 5,400 nm (base load 4,100 nm at 25 MT, max load at 150 MT), cruise 12 kts, sprint 25+ kts, max payload 150 MT — premier platform for logistics and at-sea payload deployment. Can carry four 40' ISO containers or eight 20' ISO containers.

## Hardware Features
- Durable: engineered for complex maritime operations
- Configurable & Modular: flexible architecture for mission-specific payloads and sensors
- Networked: resilient multichannel communications for manned/unmanned teaming, including contested environments
- Attritable: produced at scale for mass deployment in high-risk missions

## Software Suite
- Mission-Level Autonomy: AI and cross-platform data fusion for multi-asset teaming
- Mission Control: browser-based interface, operates independently or integrates into any operating software
- Smart Navigation: adaptive path planning optimized for various sea states
- Simulation: digital twins accelerate learning and reduce risk before physical validation
