## Summary
Oshen develops autonomous ocean robots (C-Stars) for persistent, wide-area ocean intelligence. One-meter sailing robots deployable in swarms/constellations for real-time metocean data collection. Proven in Category 5 hurricanes with NOAA. Contracts with multiple government agencies.

## Business Model
- Hardware + data services: C-Star autonomous platforms + data subscription
- B2B/B2G: government agencies (NOAA, ARIA), offshore energy, research institutions, maritime security
- Constellation-as-a-service: scalable ocean sensing networks
- Real-time data streaming via satellite to customer dashboards

## Technology

- Acoustic intelligence capabilities
