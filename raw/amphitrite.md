# Amphitrite – Raw Web Extract
**Source:** https://www.amphitrite.fr/

## Overview
Amphitrite (France) is an AI-powered ocean data intelligence company. They combine satellite technology (SWOT), real-time measurements, and predictive AI to deliver precise ocean forecasts and actionable intelligence for commercial shipping, naval defence, and maritime operations.

## Technology
- Advanced data fusion: satellite + in-situ data processed in real-time
- SWOT satellite technology integration for high-resolution ocean topography
- AI-powered predictive analytics for ocean conditions
- Ocean Bulletin routing tool

## Solutions
1. **Ocean Data Intelligence** – AI-powered maritime decision-making platform
2. **Green Shipping** – Route optimization for fuel efficiency and emissions reduction
3. **Naval Defence** – Maritime situational awareness and sovereignty

## Key Team Members
Alexandre Stegner (co-founder, oceanographer), Briac Le Vu, Evangelos Moschos, Thomas Joyce, Artemis Ioannou, and 12+ other oceanographers, data scientists, and engineers.

## Business Model
B2B SaaS: Ocean intelligence platform sold to commercial shipping, naval defence, and offshore operations. Subscription-based with per-vessel or enterprise pricing.

## Data & Technology Needs
- Real-time satellite data (SWOT, altimetry, SAR, optical)
- In-situ oceanographic measurements (temperature, salinity, currents)
- Maritime traffic data (AIS)
- Bathymetric data
- Weather and metocean forecast data
