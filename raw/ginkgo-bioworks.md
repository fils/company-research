# Ginkgo Bioworks
**Source:** https://www.ginkgo.bio/
**Sector:** Bioprospecting / Synthetic Biology
**Last Updated:** 2026-05-02

## Business Model
Autonomous labs + synthetic biology platform-as-a-service. Customers order R&D experiments/robotics via cloud/API. Revenue from platform usage (Ginkgo Solutions, Datapoints, Catalyst Agent), cell programming services for materials, enzymes, microbes (incl. marine).

## Funding & Backers
Public (DNA ticker), major clients include Pfizer, Bayer, Novo Nordisk, Corteva. Focus on scaling autonomous labs.

## Key Tech
Autonomous robotic labs, high-throughput screening, AI-guided strain engineering, "foundry" model. Marine focus: microbes & enzymes from ocean.

## Marine Data Needs
Very high for marine bioprospecting: need ocean metagenomic databases, environmental sampling data (temp, salinity, pH, nutrients, currents), biodiversity baselines to source/optimize marine-derived enzymes and microbes for industrial use.

## Highlights
Vision: program biology like computers. Strong emphasis on marine-sourced biology for novel materials, medicines.
