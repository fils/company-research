# Adaptora (Tensora Inc.) – Raw Web Extract
**Source:** VentureWell OEA Spring 2026 Stage 1 Cohort

## Business Model
Adaptora is a subsidiary brand of Tensora Inc. developing a subsurface risk intelligence platform for coastal land subsidence mitigation.

### Technology
- Ultra-high resolution ground deformation sensors for coastal monitoring
- Depth-resolved monitoring combining subsurface data streams
- Helps coastal decision-makers mitigate land subsidence risks

### Deployments
- Deploying sensor on Governors Island (NYC)
- Pilot project demonstrating real-world subsidence monitoring

### Key Info
- Brooklyn, NY
- Parent company: Tensora Inc.
- VentureWell/NOAA Ocean Enterprise Accelerator Stage 1 (Spring 2026)
- $15K TDC award
- Winner of SeaCities Adaptora / ToAptation Climate Solutions Challenge

### Sector Positioning
Falls at intersection of coastal risk, infrastructure monitoring, and climate adaptation. Provides critical subsurface data for coastal resilience planning.
