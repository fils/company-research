# Running Tide
URL: https://runningtidexmason.webflow.io/
Sector: Ocean Carbon Sequestration (Biomass sinking + OAE)

## Business Model
Ocean-based CDR leader. Uses two pathways:
1. Biomass sinking (seaweed + wood biomass to deep ocean)
2. Ocean alkalinity enhancement

Operates commercial & pilot scale deployments (Iceland main site). 25,000+ tonnes CO₂ removed and credited to date. Delivers to Microsoft, Shopify, Stripe, Patch.

## Recent Highlights
Strong MRV system with 546+ ocean sensors. Published framework protocol for responsible mCDR.
