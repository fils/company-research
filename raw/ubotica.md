**Source:** https://ubotica.com/

## Overview
Ubotica Technologies is an Irish space technology firm pioneering Orbital AI / Cognitive Earth Observation. The company upgrades the entire Earth-Observation stack with intelligence — predicting where to look, processing at the edge, and delivering decision-ready intelligence in minutes. Primary application: real-time maritime intelligence and security from space.

## Technology: SPACE:AI Platform
- **11 SPACE:AI missions successfully flown** in the harshest compute environment
- **30+ AI models deployed**: vessel detection, autonomous targeting, anomaly detection
- **Cognitive EO paradigm**: Traditional EO observes then thinks. Cognitive EO: predicts risk → decides what to task → processes at edge → learns from every cycle → delivers actionable intelligence
- **Edge AI processing**: AI runs directly on satellites, enabling near-real-time intelligence without ground station download delays
- **Proven with ESA, NASA JPL, and commercial satellite operators**

## Products & Services
- **Live Maritime Intelligence**: Subscription-based predictive surveillance that detects potential threats, tracks suspicious vessels (dark vessels, shadow fleets), and delivers decision-grade intelligence in the moment
- **SPACE:AI Platform**: Deployable AI inference at the space edge for satellite constellations
- **Space-to-seabed maritime intelligence**: Combining Cognitive EO with Fugro's global ocean infrastructure (subsea sensors, offshore vessels, USVs, UAVs) for unified maritime intelligence

## Key Differentiator
Traditional maritime surveillance is too slow, too expensive, and too rigid to detect dark vessels and shadow fleets. Ubotica's Cognitive EO approach predicts where threats will be, tasks satellites proactively, and processes AI at the edge — delivering intelligence in minutes rather than hours. The Fugro partnership extends this from space to the seabed, creating a unified maritime intelligence layer.
