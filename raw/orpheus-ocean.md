## Overview
Orpheus Ocean designs, builds, and operates autonomous underwater vehicles (AUVs) for seafloor data collection and monitoring in Earth’s most extreme environments. Small-footprint AUV for depths up to 11,000m. Enables scalable data collection for marine resources, offshore infrastructure (e.g. wind), subsea defense. $2.8M Pre-Seed funding. Partnerships with Seabed 2030, NOAA Ocean Exploration, InnovateMass ($276k award for offshore wind seafloor surveys), WHOI heritage. Eliminates need for slow vessel-dependent surveys.

## Business Model
- Cost-effective AUV access for deep-sea exploration, benthic monitoring, infrastructure inspection
- Revenue from vehicle sales/operations, data collection services, partnerships/grants
- Focus on affordable deep-sea capability previously inaccessible

### Data & Measurement Needs
- **Primary data types:** Seafloor imagery, bathymetry, environmental parameters, benthic ecosystem data, sub-bottom profiles
- **Key measurements/parameters:** Depth, temperature, salinity, currents, sediment characteristics, biodiversity indicators, polymetallic nodules/minerals
- **Observation platforms/programs:** AUV deployments for offshore wind site surveys, critical minerals exploration, defense, policy decisions; partnerships with Seabed 2030
- **Known data gaps:** High-resolution, cost-effective deep-sea data at scale; real-time or near-real-time environmental baselines for permitting and monitoring
- **Interest in external data services:** Oceanographic data integration, MRV telemetry for carbon/marine projects, multi-sensor fusion for autonomous ops
