## Overview
Jupiter Intelligence is a climate resilience platform delivering decision-grade physical risk intelligence for financial institutions, energy companies, and investors. It translates climate data into actionable financial metrics to support capital allocation, underwriting, and regulatory compliance.

Serves major Global 100 companies, largest U.S. energy & power producers, major banks. 25% of world’s largest financial institutions, 20% of Global 100, R outs largest U.S. banks.

Business Model: Climate risk analytics SaaS/platform – ClimateScore Global.
- Subscription-based for risk modeling, scenario analysis, regulatory compliance, adaptation planning.
- Provides hyperlocal (90m resolution), multi-peril, 5-year increment scenarios to 2100+.

Key for financial product development, stress testing, portfolio risk management, adaptation ROI calc.

Funding not specified on homepage (enterprise SaaS likely venture/strategic funded).

## Marine/Environmental Data Needs
High demand for marine and coastal data:
- Detailed coastal flood risk, sea level rise, storm surge modeling (AMOC collapse risk mentioned).
- Oceanographic inputs: temperature, salinity anomalies, current patterns, wave climate.
- Real-time and scenario-based climate model validation data.
- Multi-peril physical data from metocean sources (offshore energy clients like Equinor, BP).
- For infrastructure/clients: port and coastal asset data, hurricane/typhoon disruption data.
- Heavy needs for high-resolution ocean observing data (currents, sea surface temperature, salinity) and extreme event historical data.
- Interest in validation and calibration datasets, satellite observations.
- Extensive use of climate models, physico-chemical ocean data for risk predictions.

Specific needs: Marine/benthic ecosystem data less, more focused on physical risk metocean parameters for asset valuation.
