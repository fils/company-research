# Kettle
**Source:** https://ourkettle.com/
**Sector:** Climate Risk (Insurance & Underwriting)
**Last Updated:** 2026-05-02

## Business Model
AI-driven property insurance / reinsurance for climate perils (esp. wildfire). E&S lines, parametric products ("Fire in a Parcel"), reinsurance. Revenue from premiums + data licensing. Targets HOAs, high-value properties dropped by traditional insurers.

## Funding & Backers
Well-funded climate-tech insurer. Featured widely (WSJ, CNBC, Forbes).

## Key Tech
3 proprietary AI models (ignition, spread, vulnerability) trained on 130TB+ satellite/weather/real-estate data. 97% accuracy predicting burned CA properties.

## Marine Data Needs
Low direct (wildfire focus), but climate risk platform broadly benefits from integrated coastal/ocean data for compound risks (e.g. post-fire landslide + flood).

## Highlights
Predicts 97.23% of CA burned properties accurately. Helps properties in high-risk zones secure coverage.
