## Business Model
- **Hardware + autonomy platform sales**: turn-key USVs (Mariner X, Otter) + SEACONTROL navigation system retrofit for existing vessels
- **Modular payload architecture**: vessels are sensor-agnostic platforms that accept customer-specific payloads
- **Dual-use commercial+defense**: serves industrial (offshore energy, hydrography, environmental, fisheries) and defense (mine countermeasures, maritime security) markets
- **Fleet-level deployment strategy**: customers shifting from pilot projects to fleet-level — revenue growing 5x in 5 years, 2x in 2 years
