## Company Overview
Pronoe is a French ocean carbon removal company developing automated water treatment systems that restore the ocean's natural capacity to permanently remove CO2 from the air. Their approach is asset-light, leveraging existing industrial infrastructure.

**Location:** France
**Mission:** Restoring the ocean's natural ability to capture and permanently store CO2 from the air, in a sustainable, scalable and asset-light manner

## Technology & Solution
- **Automated water treatment systems** integrated downstream from industrial sites
- Uses electricity to process return flows to the sea into less acidic or more alkaline flows
- Compliant with current applicable regulations
- Leverages third-party infrastructure (no dedicated marine deployment vessels needed)
- Issues high-quality CO2 removal certificates
- Effectively mitigates coastal acidification
- Asset-light model: no need for dedicated ocean-based infrastructure

## Approach
- Oceans are the biggest carbon sinks and best allies for CO2 removal at scale
- Ocean removal capacity is decreasing due to stress and acidification
- Pronoe restores this natural capacity by processing industrial outflows
- Uses electrochemical alkalinity enhancement on existing industrial discharge streams

## Business Model
- Sells CO2 removal certificates (carbon credits)
- Licenses/deploys automated water treatment systems at industrial sites
- Asset-light: leverages existing industrial infrastructure rather than building dedicated marine operations
- Industrial site partners benefit from compliance with discharge regulations and carbon removal credits

## Key Differentiators
- **Asset-light**: No dedicated marine vessels or offshore platforms needed
- **Co-located with industry**: Integrated into existing industrial outflows
- **Coastal acidification mitigation**: Direct environmental co-benefit
- **Scalable**: Can be deployed at any industrial site with coastal discharge
