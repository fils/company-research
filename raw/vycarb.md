# Vycarb
**Source:** https://vycarb.com/
**Sector:** Ocean Carbon + Aquaculture Environment Monitoring
**Last Updated:** 2026-05-02

## Business Model
Technology provider (not project developer). Full-stack autonomous carbon capture/storage + real-time sensing in water bodies (ocean, aquaculture). Passive flow pH-mediated bicarbonate storage + proprietary sensors for instant, measurable, permanent CO₂ removal. Revenue: tech licensing, hardware sales, verified carbon credits. Customers: Klarna, DOE, Frontier, Stripe, Milkywire.

## Funding & Backers
Strong: Rio Tinto, Shell Ventures, NYSERDA, Sea Ahead, BlueScope, Impact Science, Gigascale, etc. Backed by carbon chemists + naval engineers.

## Key Tech
pH-mediated CO2 neutralization to HCO3, real-time carbon sensing, autonomous in-water deployment, full MRV stack.

## Marine Data Needs
Core competency: Real-time carbonate chemistry (pH, DIC, alkalinity, pCO2), ecosystem impact monitoring, sensor networks for MRV. Explicitly designed for measurable ocean alkalinity-style CDR in marine/aquaculture settings. High demand/ supply of marine telemetry.

## Highlights
Validated since 2023. Deployments in NYC waters. Scalable for any water; solves verification skepticism with direct sensing.
