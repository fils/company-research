# Vaulted Deep - Raw Web Extract (2026-05-01)

**Source:** https://vaulteddeep.com/

## Overview
Dual waste management + carbon removal company. Takes organic waste (manure, sewage sludge, paper sludge, agricultural byproducts) that would otherwise pollute and injects it deep underground for permanent geological storage (10,000+ years) using oil & gas derived technology.

**Carbon Removal Impact (as of extract)**: 44,415 tonnes CO₂ removed. Certified by Isometric.

## Business Model
- Waste-to-value carbon removal service
- Accepts and disposes of challenging organic wastes
- Generates revenue from:
  - Carbon credit sales (verified permanent removal)
  - Waste disposal fees
- Targets high-volume waste producers (feedlots, municipalities, industry)
- Large-scale buyers of removal credits: Stripe, Autodesk, Frontier, H&M, JP Morgan, Alphabet, Meta, Shopify, Watershed, Workday

## Technology
- Deep injection into geological formations (5,000+ feet)
- Proven oil & gas infrastructure repurposed for waste disposal and carbon storage
- Permanent sequestration
