## Summary
Apeiron Labs is enabling the digital transformation of oceans — from episodic sampling to continuous observing, from expendability to reusability, and from human-assisted to autonomous sensing. Focus on persistent upper ocean data collection using low-cost AUVs.

## Business Model
- Data-as-a-Service (DaaS) for ocean intelligence
- Autonomous underwater vehicle (AUV) fleet operations
- Low-cost-per-data-point sensing model
- B2B/B2G sales to climate tech, offshore energy, government research agencies

## Technology
- Tensor platform: cost-effective autonomous underwater vehicles
- Low-cost, reusable AUVs for persistent ocean sensing
- 4D subsurface temperature and salinity observations
- Significant model performance improvement over satellite SST alone

## Funding & Backers
- $9.5M Series A (February 2026) led by Dyne Ventures
- Also backed by TFX Capital, F4 Fund, Assembly Ventures
- SBIR grants from US government

## Marine Data & Measurement Needs — CORE BUSINESS
- Primary data types: subsurface temperature, salinity profiles, oceanographic physical parameters
- Upper ocean (0-200m) continuous monitoring — the company's entire business model
- Autonomous sensor networks replacing costly ship-based surveys
- Real-time ocean intelligence for climate modeling, hurricane forecasting, offshore operations
- Key measurements: temperature, salinity, depth, currents (CTD-type profiles)
- Addresses critical gap: 4D subsurface observations have 4X more impact on ocean models than SST-only
- Enables persistent sensing at dramatically lower carbon footprint than traditional methods
- Ideal data partner for CDR MRV, offshore wind site characterization, climate risk modeling
