## Company Overview
Tampa Deep Sea Xplorers (TDSX) is radically improving the cost effectiveness of underwater exploration and data collection. Their Barracuda AUV is designed to go deeper, enable greater maneuverability, collect more data in less time, and provide a lower cost solution. The company is actively commercializing — accepting orders for Barracuda.

## Target Industries & Applications
- **Offshore Energy** – Oil & Gas, wind, wave power generation; underwater robotics for inspection, maintenance, survey
- **Oceanographic Research** – Sub-bottom profiling, water column characterization, ocean current profiles, tides, temperature, salinity, water clarity, bio-luminescence, ocean pollution
- **Military & Security** – Subsea operations for Navy, Coastal Security, advanced defense missions
