# Nexus Ocean AI – Raw Web Extract
**Source:** https://nexusoceanai.com/

## Business Model
Nexus Ocean AI is an AI-native operating system for the maritime industry. Its SAM platform automates maritime workflows and preserves institutional knowledge through role-specific AI personas ("Avatars").

### Three Native Products
- **SAVITAR**: Knowledge & Control Tower – fleet tracking, voyage management, CII compliance modeling, automated reporting
- **SAVANT**: Systems & Workflow Engine – API integration with existing VMS/ERP; orchestrates cross-department workflows autonomously
- **SeAMate**: Email & Communication – AI-drafted maritime correspondence, port arrival reports, real-time email triage

### Role-Specific AI Advisors
- Operations: Voyage planning, port coordination
- QHSE: Safety audits, ISM/ISPS standards
- Commercial: Chartering strategy, freight, laytime
- Compliance: Regulatory oversight (CII & IMO)
- Technical: Defect management, PMS

### Technical Architecture
- Self-hosted model (AWS, Azure, or GCP) – runs on client's own tenancy
- Data sovereignty: prompts/data don't become third-party training data
- Knowledge graph built from 2-3 years of operational mailbox data (PST/MBOX)
