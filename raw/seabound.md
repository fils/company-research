# Seabound - Raw Web Extract (2026-05-01)

**Source:** https://www.seabound.co/

## Overview
Seabound provides a **retrofittable onboard carbon capture system** for ships. Captures 25–95% of CO₂ emissions from vessel exhaust using an abundant, regenerable sorbent. System is modular, low-CAPEX, automated, and remotely monitored.

## Business Model
- Retrofit service for existing cargo and shipping fleets
- Revenue model combines hardware installation with ongoing capture and offloading logistics
- Targets shipowners facing strict new maritime emissions regulations (IMO, EU, etc.)
- Partners with major ship operators and receives funding from Y Combinator, Lowercarbon Capital, and others

## Technology
- Onboard capture unit installed on ship
- Uses abundant, low-cost sorbent
- CO₂ is captured during voyages and offloaded at ports as carbonate
- Captured carbonate can be recycled or used in cement/fuel production
