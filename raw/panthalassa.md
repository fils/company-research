## Summary
Panthalassa is building a planetary-scale clean energy platform from the middle of the ocean. Wave-powered energy generation enabling offshore AI computing/data centers. $140M Series B (May 2026). Public-benefit corporation. Ocean-2 pilot deployment in Pacific.

## Business Model
- Clean energy generation via wave power — sell energy/compute at sea
- Offshore AI data center hosting and compute services
- Renewable energy certificates and carbon offsets
- Infrastructure-as-a-service for offshore compute

## Technology
- Wave energy conversion platform
- Offshore compute nodes integrated with renewable energy generation
- Ocean-2 pilot deployment series in northern Pacific
- Team from SpaceX, Google, Blue Origin, NASA, Boeing, Amazon, Tesla, Apple
