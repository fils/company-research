## Summary
Ulysses (legal entity "The Ocean Company") builds small, low-cost, modular autonomous underwater vehicles (AUVs) and surface/subsea systems ("Kraken" launch & recovery system) for marine data acquisition, ecosystem restoration, and defense missions. Vertically integrated hardware + software; vehicles are 50x cheaper than legacy providers and can operate in swarms. $46M total raised in seed + Series A led by a16z American Dynamism fund (April 2026). US Navy, Mote Marine Lab, VIMS, Great Barrier Reef Foundation, The Nature Conservancy customers.

## Business Model
- Sale/lease of Mako AUVs to government, research, and commercial customers
- "As-a-service" autonomous missions for subsea survey, inspection, intervention
- Defense contracts (US Department of War, allied navies)
- Ecosystem restoration services (seagrass planting, reef monitoring)
- Vertically integrated manufacturing in San Francisco (lower unit cost as moat)

## Technology
- **Mako AUV** — 72hr endurance, 200 lb max payload, 5,000 ft depth rating
- **Kraken** — autonomous launch, recovery, and recharge system (zero human intervention)
- Modular "Lego" design — swappable payloads, batteries, compute, sensors via standardized ports
- Cooperative swarming — vehicles intercommunicate; stealthy low-bandwidth comms
- 40x more subsea computing power than small/medium vehicle competitors
- Advanced navigation for GPS/comm-denied environments
