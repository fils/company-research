# Ebb Carbon
URL: https://www.ebbcarbon.com/
Sector: Ocean Carbon Sequestration
Last Updated: 2026-05-01

## Business Model
Pioneers electrochemical water processing integrated with desalination plants. Converts waste brine into:
- Additional freshwater recovery
- Permanent ocean alkalinity enhancement carbon removal
- Valuable acid/caustic soda industrial products

Target customers: Desalination operators. Monetization via capital equipment sales + operating services + sale of CDR credits and chemicals.

Key Projects: Project Macoma (Port Angeles, WA with US DOE PNNL), Google collaboration announced 2025.

## Funding & Investors
Partners/clients: Microsoft, Stripe (CDR purchases), Isometric (verification). Multiple US DOE-backed pilots. No specific financing round disclosed in latest site update.

## Key Tech
Electrochemical membrane stacks (modular). High energy efficiency. Ocean-safe alkalinity addition for acidification mitigation. Third-party verification required.
