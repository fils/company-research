## Summary
Vatn Systems is a defense-technology company building affordable, modular autonomous underwater vehicles (AUVs) that operate in cooperative swarms for naval warfare, subsea surveillance, research, and commercial maritime missions. Rhode Island–based; founded 2023 by maritime experts and military leaders. $60M Series A (December 2025) — one of the largest AUV defense raises to date. First international contract win in Singapore.

## Business Model
- Sale/lease of attritable (low-cost, expendable) AUVs to defense customers
- Cooperative swarm mission services
- R&D contracts with US Navy, allied navies, research institutions
- Modular payload licensing for third-party sensor integration
- International defense sales (e.g., Singapore)

## Technology
- **Modular AUV platform** — flexible deployment by hand, air, or surface vessel
- **Advanced navigation software** — effective in GPS/vision/comms-denied environments
- **Autonomous, "humans optional"** — full autonomy, no human-in-the-loop
- **Cooperative swarming** — vehicles sync in time/space for complex missions
- **Stealthy low-bandwidth comms** between vehicles
- **Advanced path planning** for collaborative task completion
- **Sensor/data fusion** techniques that reduce error in inertial navigation
- Modular building block structure — tailored to range, accuracy, sensor payload
