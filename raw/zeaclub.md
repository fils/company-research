# Zeaclub – Raw Web Extract
**Source:** https://www.zeaclub.com/

## Company Overview
Zeaclub brings every stakeholder — shipowner, shipbroker, ship charterer, agent — and every document and decision into one intelligent shared workspace. AI-powered maritime operations simplification.

## Value Propositions
- **Faster exchange** – Real-time collaboration replaces email chains and spreadsheet ping-pong
- **Fewer mistakes** – Structured data entry and validation eliminate costly manual errors
- **Lower cost** – Streamlined workflows reduce admin overhead and dispute resolution time

## Problems Solved
1. **Scattered data** – Fixture details, port schedules, cost breakdowns live across dozens of emails, spreadsheets, and PDFs
2. **Manual data entry** – Same voyage details re-typed into multiple systems, wasting hours daily
3. **Costly disputes** – No shared record causes demurrage, laytime, freight calculation disagreements
4. **Slow approvals** – Invoices and statements bounce between inboxes, delaying cash flow

## Platform Capabilities
- Parties & contacts management
- Vessel details
- Charter terms
- Port timeline
- Costs & calculations
- Invoices & statements
- Laytime calculations, freight invoices, demurrage claims from shared workspace

## Users by Role
- **Charterers** – Manage voyage costs, track laytime, approve invoices/claims
- **Shipowners** – Share vessel specs, monitor freight and demurrage
- **Brokers** – Coordinate fixtures, keep all parties aligned, generate recaps
- **Operators** – Track port rotations/ETAs, log events, collaborate
- **Finance Teams** – Review/approve invoices, reconcile costs, export to accounting

## Security
- Role-based permissions
- Full audit trail with timestamps
- Private isolated workspaces
- Encrypted at rest and in transit
