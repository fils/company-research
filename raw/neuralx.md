## Company Overview
NeuralX provides "decision intelligence for aquaculture" — AI-powered analytics that turn underwater camera feeds into actionable decisions on feeding, harvest timing, and fish health. Built for intensive salmon, Kanpachi, mackerel, and Tiger Prawn operations doing $5M+ in annual revenue.

**Location:** Los Angeles, California

**Company Size:** 2-10 employees (LinkedIn)

## Products & Platform

### Core AI Platform
- Real-time fish counting from existing underwater cameras (no hardware replacement needed)
- Biomass estimation with 92%+ accuracy
- Body length measurement: 0.43cm error, 1.89g weight error (validated at active pilot)
- Size grading & feed optimization recommendations
- Works for Kanpachi, Mackerel, Salmon, Tiger Prawn

### Key Features
1. **Biomass Estimation** — Non-contact daily assessments validated against manual ground-truth sampling
2. **Satiation Detection** — AI identifies when fish stop eating by analyzing mouth movements, swimming patterns, feed density; stops 10-15% overfeeding
3. **Early Health Detection** — Behavioral anomalies (lethargy, abnormal swimming, reduced feeding response) flagged 2-5 days before visible symptoms
4. **Harvest Optimization** — Monitors 100% of stock continuously, reduces biomass uncertainty from ±15% to ±7%
5. **Automated Compliance Reporting** — ASC/BAP ready, auto-logs feeding events, mortality alerts, environmental observations
6. **Camera Integration** — Works with existing standard underwater IP cameras and CCTV infrastructure; 2-4 week deployment

### Deep Technology
- **Artificial Life Simulation Engine**: Proprietary 3D synthetic training data pipeline that generates unlimited training data including rare events (disease outbreaks, extreme weather, equipment failures)
- **Hybrid Density + Detection Model**: Three complementary strategies (density estimation, bounding-box, point-based) combined adaptively
- **World Model + Decision Layer**: Simulates outcomes of changes to feeding policy, harvest timing, stocking density
- Dataset available at nere-us.com
- Based on peer-reviewed research: DeepFoids (NeurIPS 2022) — adaptive bio-inspired fish simulation using deep reinforcement learning
