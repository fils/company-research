## High Integrity Carbon Removal

Accelerating natural processes to mitigate climate change.

pHathom accelerates nature’s carbon cycle by capturing CO₂ from biomass power plants, dissolving it in seawater, and neutralizing it with limestone — creating durable calcium bicarbonate for ocean carbon storage. The process supports marine life by countering acidification through calcium carbonate benefits.

### Key Benefits (explicitly marketed)
- **Scalable & Cost-Effective** — low energy, modular plants bolted onto existing coastal biomass infrastructure.
- **Directly Measurable** — every stage documented and traceable; MRV partners include Statolith (sustainable biomass) and Aquatic Labs (post-release bay monitoring of pH and total alkalinity to confirm no post-release CO₂).
- **Low Energy Consumption** — significantly less energy than traditional amine systems.
- **Minimal Infrastructure Required** — no large wells, pipelines or geologic storage needed. Uses once-through cooling at coastal sites; modular builds.
- **Durable Carbon Storage** — bicarbonate stable for >10,000 years.
- **Supports Marine Life** — mimics natural weathering; replenishes calcium bicarbonate needed for shell/coral growth, helping de-acidify coastal waters.

## Business Model & Sector
- **Sector**: Ocean Carbon Sequestration (BECCS ocean-storage pathway + ocean alkalinity co-benefits; Accelerated Weathering of Limestone).
- **Core Offer**: High-integrity CDR credits from coastal biomass plants; integration of carbon-capture-on-bioenergy with ocean storage. Commercialization target 2030; potential GT/yr scale.
- **Funding**: $4M Seed (Feb 2026) led by Propeller Ventures with NBIF, Invest Nova Scotia, Carmeuse Ventures etc. → total committed capital >$12M (seed equity + grants).

## Key Technology
- **Process**: Capture CO₂ from biomass combustion; convert CO₂ + limestone + seawater → bicarbonate → ocean discharge. Accelerates natural mineral weathering.
- **Innovation vs traditional CCS**: No compression, pipelines, injection wells. Coastal siting eliminates geologic storage needs. Very low energy.
- **Validation**: Partnership with Aquatic Labs for basin-scale water quality + carbon flux monitoring post-release. Onshore pH & alkalinity measurements quantify capture.
- **Scale potential**: Hundreds of millions to billions of tons/yr (IPCC-relevant).
