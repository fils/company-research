### Overview
Sea Machines Robotics develops autonomous control and navigation systems for commercial and defense vessels. Boston, MA based. Dual-use by design — trusted by both commercial and defense customers. Export-ready, with production in the US and Europe.

### Products
**Autonomy Systems:**
- **SM300-SP** — Attritable and compact autonomy system
- **SM300-NG** — Class-approved and robust autonomy system
- Designed for rapid deployment across vessel sizes from 10 to 300 ft
- Operates in GPS-denied and communications-denied environments
- One-box solution for cost-effective autonomy at scale

**Unmanned Surface Vehicles (USVs):**
- **SELKIE** — Versatile and modular USV
- **STORMRUNNER** — Built for contested waters (AUSV)

**Software Tools:**
- SM Link Streaming-API
- SM Link Control-API
- FleetViewer

**Computer Vision:**
- **AI-ris** — 4K computer vision system

### Business Model
Hardware (autonomy systems + USVs) sales + software subscriptions (SM Link APIs, FleetViewer). Dual-use commercial + defense revenue streams. Retrofits existing vessels and integrates with new builds. Export-ready with US and Europe production facilities.

### Data & Measurement Needs
- **Primary data types**: Real-time vessel sensor streams (GPS, IMU, compass, radar, AIS), computer vision feeds (AI-ris 4K cameras), environment sensors, control telemetry
- **Key measurements/parameters**: Vessel position, heading, speed, attitude, obstacle detection/classification, sea state, wind, wave height, GPS signal integrity (spoofing/jamming detection)
- **Observation platforms/programs**: Vessel-mounted autonomy systems (SM300), USV missions (SELKIE, STORMRUNNER), fleet-level remote monitoring (FleetViewer)
- **Known data gaps**: GPS-denied environment operations require alternative navigation sources (inertial, visual); limited autonomous navigation in extreme weather
- **Interest in external data services**: Hydrographic charts, real-time weather/oceanographic feeds, satellite communications for beyond-line-of-sight USV control
