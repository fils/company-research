**Source:** https://www.tidalx.ai/en

## Company Overview
Tidal (TidalX AI Inc.) is an AI-powered underwater vision and robotics company for aquaculture. Originally developed within Alphabet's X (the moonshot factory), Tidal graduated as an independent company in August 2024. Mountain View, CA HQ with Bergen, Norway office.

## Mission
Support sustainable growth in aquaculture and protect the ocean. Seafood is a primary protein source for 3B+ people, but 90% of global fish stocks are depleted. Tidal brings AI to aquaculture enabling a more sustainable seafood industry with healthier fish and thriving oceans.

## Product: Orca Camera System
Tidal's integrated platform combines autonomous robotic cameras, precision sensors, and enterprise-ready AI.

- Real-time **biomass estimation**
- **Sea lice detection**
- **Fish welfare monitoring**
- **Autonomous feeding**
- NS9415 hardware certification (first AI camera system with this Norwegian standard)
- Google Cloud infrastructure
- Multiple built-in cameras
- Seamless over-the-air updates
- Enterprise-grade platform built with Google's robust security standards

## AI & Machine Learning
- AI models trained through extensive partnership with Mowi (world's largest Atlantic salmon producer)
- Refined by testing in hundreds of commercial pens across diverse geographies and extreme environmental conditions
- "Fishal recognition" — individual fish tracking and identification
- Fish behavior detection and ocean-simulation modeling
- Orca newest generation underwater camera launched 2026
