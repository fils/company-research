# MacroBreed – Raw Web Extract
**Source:** https://macrobreed.com/

## Business Model
MacroBreed pioneers sustainable kelp breeding using cutting-edge genomic solutions. They unlock the vast potential of kelp for sustainable aquaculture, biofuel production, and carbon capture.

### Key Metrics
- **300% increase** in kelp yield demonstrated in breeding trials using genomic selection
- **$11M+** in competitive grants (ARPA-E, USDA SBIR)
- **1st patented non-reproductive kelp strain** (PCT/US2024/027903) — enables expansion of safe ocean farming zones

### Research Highlights
- **Genome Assembly & Annotation**: Published reference genomes for Macrocystis pyrifera (giant kelp) and Saccharina latissima (sugar kelp), available on JGI PhycoCosm
- **Non-Reproductive Kelp**: High-impact mutations in genes essential for reproduction — sporeless strains for safe, scalable ocean farming
- **Seed Banks & Elite Cultivars**: Diverse kelp seed banks, high-performance cultivars bred for yield, environmental resilience, consistency
