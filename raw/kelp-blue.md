**Source:** https://www.kelp.blue/

## Summary
Kelp Blue cultivates Giant Kelp (Macrocystis pyrifera) on large-scale offshore structures for carbon sequestration, ocean rewilding, and natural biostimulant production (StimBlue+). Global operations across USA, Africa, Asia, Europe, Latam, Pacific.

## Business Model
- B2B agricultural biostimulant sales (StimBlue+) to farmers
- Blue carbon credits via kelp sequestration
- Revenue from sustainable agriculture inputs
- 14+ crop field trials with proven ROI

## Technology
- Patented cell-wall bursting technology for biostimulant extraction
- Offshore giant kelp cultivation structures
- No land, fresh water, pesticides or fertilizers needed
- Kelp sustainably harvestable for 7 years
- Target: 0.2-0.5 Gt CO₂/year sequestration by 2050
