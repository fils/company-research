# Hullbot – Raw Web Extract
**Source:** https://www.hullbot.com/

## Overview
Hullbot builds autonomous underwater robots that provide frequent hull cleaning and inspection services for commercial and recreational fleets. The proactive "grooming" approach prevents biofouling buildup, reducing drag and fuel consumption while increasing fleet performance. The company operates a cloud robotics platform with real-time analytics.

## Technology & Platform
- **Autonomous underwater robots**: Advanced underwater robotics platform with powerful magnetic grip for hull adhesion
- **Gentle brush system**: Safe for all coatings (including silicone); cleaning speed, pressures, and brush type adjusted per vessel
- **Cloud robotics**: Real-time analytics and fleet performance data recording and analysis
- **Manoeuvrability**: Unparalleled manoeuvrability enabling cleaning of propellers and niche areas
- **Day and night operations**: Flexible scheduling with simple deployment, no service disruptions

## Business Model
- **Autonomous frequent cleaning as-a-service**: No upfront cost to vessel operators
- **Proactive grooming vs reactive cleaning**: Paradigm shift — cleaning early and often prevents meaningful fouling before it occurs
- **Global service**: Delivering services globally with international hubs
- **Asset intelligence**: Recording and analyzing data on fleet performance and hull condition over time
