# First Street Foundation

**Sector**: Climate Risk

**Official Site**: https://firststreet.org/

**Overview**  
First Street Foundation is the recognized standard for property-level climate risk financial modeling. It uses transparent, physics-based deterministic models to calculate risk and potential financial impact on real estate worldwide.

**Core Capabilities**  
- Property-level risk scores and loss modeling (flood, wildfire, wind, heat)  
- Damage and downtime simulation based on actual building characteristics  
- Global coverage with data used by Fannie Mae, Zillow, major banks, insurers, and government agencies

**Business Model**  
Data licensing and enterprise platform (asset due diligence, portfolio management, strategy/reporting).

**Marine Relevance**  
Extremely relevant for coastal properties, ports, and marine-adjacent real estate due to strong flood and sea-level rise modeling.

**Recognition**  
Featured in Fast Company’s 2026 Most Innovative Companies list.
