# Apeiron Labs

**Sector**: Ocean Carbon Sequestration / Ocean Data
**Official Site**: https://www.apeironlabs.com/

**Overview**
Apeiron Labs is building the digital transformation of ocean monitoring through autonomous underwater vehicles (AUVs). Their Tensor platform deploys low-cost, reusable AUVs for persistent upper-ocean (0-200m) monitoring, replacing expensive ship-based surveys with continuous autonomous sensing at dramatically lower carbon footprint.

**Technology**
- Tensor AUV platform — low-cost autonomous underwater vehicles
- 4D subsurface temperature and salinity profiling
- Fleet-based distributed sensor networks
- Edge-to-cloud data delivery

**Business Model**
Data-as-a-Service (DaaS) for ocean intelligence. B2B/B2G customers in climate tech, offshore energy, and government research.

**Recent Highlights**
- $9.5M Series A (Feb 2026) led by Dyne Ventures
- Also backed by TFX Capital, F4 Fund, Assembly Ventures
- SBIR grants from US government

### Data & Measurement Needs
- Primary data types: Subsurface temperature, salinity, current profiles
- Key measurements: Temperature (0-200m), salinity, depth, current velocity
- Observation platforms: Tensor AUVs, distributed fleet networks
- Known data gaps: Persistent subsurface observations remain globally scarce; traditional ship surveys too costly
- Interest in external data services: Core business — Apeiron Labs provides the very upper-ocean monitoring data that CDR/MRV programs need
