# Quartermaster

**Sector**: Ocean Data & AI
**Official Site**: https://www.quartermaster.us/

## Overview
Quartermaster is an Arlington, Virginia–based maritime-technology startup building **SmartMast™**, a distributed maritime sensing and AI analytics network mounted on commercial vessels. Described as a continuous sensing layer that fills gaps in maritime awareness vessel-by-vessel — positioned as an upgrade over fraud-prone AIS (opt-in, self-reported, spoofable). CEO **Neil Sobin**.

## Technology
- **SmartMast™** — vessel-mounted sensor package (cameras + radios) on working vessels:
  - Optical + IR capture; dual software-defined radios (AIS, ADS-B, VHF, radar)
  - Onboard AI processing (vessel detection, classification, tracking)
  - Low-latency satcom; tens of GB/day per mast
- **AI analytics platform** — multimodal AI for incident detection, vessel ID, activity patterns
- Real-time feeds for insurers, fleets, energy operators, governments

## Business Model
- **Pro-mariner network effect**: free hardware + install + support + own-feed access + hazard alerts + broadband at sea in exchange for mast space and data contribution
- Subscription / data products for insurers, commercial fleets, energy, defense MDA
- Training-data adjacency for marine autonomy developers
- Physical presence thesis vs pure AIS/satellite/RF remote sensing

### Data & Measurement Needs
- **Primary data types:** Vessel tracking; HD/IR imagery; AIS/ADS-B/VHF/radar capture; edge AI classifications; satcom telemetry; crowdsourced MDA; multi-modal fusion
- **Key measurements/parameters:** Position, heading, speed; vessel class; RF emissions; radar bearing; neighbor relative intent; anomaly flags
- **Observation platforms/programs:** Vessel-mounted SmartMast fleet; global satcom; centralized analytics
- **Known data gaps:** Independent vessel ID (AIS spoofable); undersea layer fusion (see [Andrenam](andrenam.md)); high-res chokepoint risk pricing for insurers
- **Interest in external data services:** High — weather/oceanographic feeds, undersea acoustics, satellite RF/optical (Unseenlabs/Ubotica) as complementary channels
