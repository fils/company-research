# Nexus Ocean AI

**Sector**: Maritime Operations & Analytics
**Official Site**: https://nexusoceanai.com/
**Last Updated**: 2026-05-24

## Business Model
Nexus Ocean AI provides an AI-native operating system for the maritime industry, automating up to 90% of maritime workflows through genAI personas (SAM — your Maritime AI Persona) augmented by a Maritime Language Model. They target eliminating repetitive "grunt work" in shipping, port operations, and logistics.

## Key Technology
- Maritime Language Model (domain-specific LLM)
- SAM — Maritime AI Persona service orchestration
- Automated maritime workflows (shipping documentation, port logistics, compliance)
- Global reach (operating across multiple continents)

## Data & Measurement Needs
- Primary data types: Maritime operational data (shipping manifests, port schedules, vessel tracking), regulatory compliance data, logistics coordination records, Maritime Language Model training corpora
- Key measurements/parameters: Port turnaround times, shipping route efficiency metrics, cargo clearance times, fuel consumption data, emissions tracking
- Observation platforms/programs: AIS vessel tracking systems, port community systems, customs documentation platforms, maritime weather services
- Known data gaps: Fragmented maritime data across different port authorities; inconsistent data standards across jurisdictions; real-time vessel status visibility gaps
- Interest in external data services: Global AIS data feeds, port congestion indices, maritime weather/ocean routing data, international trade flow statistics
