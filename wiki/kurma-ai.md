## Kurma AI

**Overview**: Kurma AI (San Francisco, CA) builds domain-specific generative AI and computer vision for aquaculture and fisheries. Their technology stack spans foundational language models (AQUA-7B), conversational AI (AquaChat), autonomous operations (AQUA OS), and compliance monitoring (AquaEye).

**Funding**: VentureWell/NOAA Ocean Enterprise Accelerator Stage 0 (Spring 2026). Partners: NVIDIA Inception, Google for Startups, Nebius.

**Key Technology**:
- **AQUA-7B**: 7-billion parameter LLM for the global aquaculture industry (on Hugging Face)
- **AquaChat**: World's first free AI assistant for aquaculture farmers. Voice-enabled in 13+ languages. Available on Web, iOS, and Android.
- **AQUA OS**: Agentic AI platform for autonomous aquaculture operations. Real-time dashboard showing pH, temperature, DO, NH₃, device control (aerator, feeder, pump), camera feeds, and agent activity logging.
- **AquaEye**: Computer vision for fisheries security and compliance.

**Data & Measurement Needs**:
- Primary data types: Real-time sensor telemetry (pH, DO, temperature, NH₃), underwater camera feeds (fish behavior, health, feeding), aquaculture operational data, multi-lingual aquaculture knowledge corpus
- Key measurements/parameters: pH, dissolved oxygen (DO), temperature, NH₃ (ammonia), fish biomass via CV, feed consumption rates, fish behavior patterns
- Observation platforms/programs: Continuous pond/farm sensor networks (24/7 telemetry), underwater camera monitoring, agentic AI control loops (auto-adjust aerator/feeder/pump), mobile-first data collection (WhatsApp alerts, phone-based reporting)
- Known data gaps: Standardized aquaculture datasets for model training, cross-species fish health models, real-time water quality from remote/small farms, multilingual knowledge bases
- Interest in external data services: High — AQUA models require large-scale aquaculture data; actively seeking partnerships with farms, research institutions, and sensor providers

**Cross-sector relevance**: Shares aquaculture AI space with **ReelData AI** (land-based, $8M Series A) and **Aquabyte** (computer vision/biomass). Kurma AI is distinguished by its GenAI/LLM approach and multi-language accessibility.
