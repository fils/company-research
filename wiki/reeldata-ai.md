# ReelData AI

**Sector**: Aquaculture
**Official Site**: https://www.reeldata.ai/

**Overview**
ReelData AI provides a comprehensive AI-driven software suite for land-based Recirculating Aquaculture Systems (RAS). Their platform automates feeding, biomass estimation, health monitoring, and stress detection, helping farms maximize growth while minimizing costs and environmental impact.

**Technology**
- ReelAppetite: Automated AI feeding based on real-time appetite
- ReelWeight: Non-intrusive biomass estimation via AI imaging
- ReelVision: Custom AI camera for behavior and feeding analysis
- ReelStress: Real-time stress detection from feeding + water quality
- ReelHealth: Early disease and maturation detection

**Business Model**
SaaS software licensing + AI camera hardware (ReelVision). Per-farm pricing for aquaculture operators globally.

- Total raised: ~$10.9M

### Data & Measurement Needs
- Primary data types: Tank water quality data, fish behavior imaging, feed consumption data
- Key measurements: Temperature, pH, dissolved oxygen, salinity, fish appetite indicators, growth rates
- Observation platforms: ReelVision AI cameras, in-tank environmental sensors
- Known data gaps: Continuous real-time water quality data at sufficient granularity
- Interest in external data services: High — ReelStress and ReelHealth depend on integrated water quality data streams
