# Nearview LLC

**Sector**: Aquaculture
**Official Site**: https://nearviewllc.com/
**Last Updated**: 2026-05-24

## Business Model
Nearview LLC develops multi-object detection AI for identifying lobster buoys, boats, and aquaculture gear from aerial and satellite imagery. Their technology enables marine-user deconfliction — helping different ocean users (commercial fishers, aquaculture operators, recreational boaters) avoid conflicts over shared marine spaces.

## Funding
- **VentureWell/NOAA OEA Stage 1** (Spring 2026) — $15K TDC award; in-person workshop at University of Rhode Island Bay Campus (June 2026)

## Key Technology
- **Multi-Object Detection AI**: Simultaneous identification of lobster buoys, fishing boats, and aquaculture gear from overhead imagery
- **Aerial Image Analysis**: Processing drone and aircraft imagery for marine object detection
- **Satellite Image Analysis**: Detecting marine infrastructure from space-based platforms
- **Marine Deconfliction Mapping**: Identifying overlapping use areas and conflict hotspots

## Data & Measurement Needs
- **Primary data types**: Aerial imagery (drone, aircraft), satellite imagery, marine spatial data, vessel AIS tracking data, fisheries management zone boundaries
- **Key measurements/parameters**: Lobster buoy GPS positions, boat traffic density, aquaculture lease boundaries, spatial overlap zones, conflict incident locations
- **Observation platforms/programs**: Aerial survey flights, satellite imagery acquisition (high-resolution optical), AIS vessel tracking networks
- **Known data gaps**: Real-time buoy location tracking; automated detection of submerged or partially-submerged gear; consistent satellite revisit times for dynamic fishing areas; integration of tribal and commercial fishing ground knowledge
- **Interest in external data services**: NOAA fisheries management zone data, USCG vessel traffic data, state lobster commission buoy registry data, satellite imagery providers (Planet, Maxar), AIS data aggregators
