## Data & Measurement Needs

- **Primary data types**: Seawater alkalinity, pH, DIC, trace metal chemistry, ecosystem monitoring data, discharge water chemistry from municipal/industrial outfalls
- **Key measurements/parameters**: Total alkalinity, pH, DIC, calcium carbonate saturation state, trace metal concentrations (nickel, vanadium from alkaline minerals), plankton community composition, dissolved oxygen, nutrient levels
- **Observation platforms/programs**: Collaborative monitoring with Dalhousie University and National Oceanography Centre (UK); partner lab networks for OAE protocol validation; multi-parameter water sampling at Tufts Cove outfall
- **Known data gaps**: In-situ high-frequency alkalinity sensors (currently rely on discrete sampling); lack of standardized OAE monitoring protocols accepted across verification bodies; limited ecosystem impact baselines at coastal outfall sites
- **Interest in external data services**: Critical — OAE verification requires comprehensive water chemistry monitoring; needs coastal sensor network data for baseline characterization
