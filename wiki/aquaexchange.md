# AquaExchange

**Sector**: Aquaculture
**Official Site**: https://aquaexchange.com/

## Overview
Aqua Exchange is transforming shrimp aquaculture with a comprehensive IoT/AI platform that combines farm automation, AI analytics, marketplace, embedded finance, and insurance into one smart ecosystem. Founded 2020 in Vijayawada, India, the company has deployed 20,000 devices across 80,000 acres in 4 countries, serving the shrimp farming industry for over 5 years. AquaExchange's "One Smart Ecosystem" philosophy connects every pond, automates every action, and optimizes every harvest.

## Business Model
- **Full-stack platform**: IoT hardware + AI analytics + farm management software + marketplace + embedded finance + insurance
- **Hardware + Software SaaS**: Connected ecosystem approach with proprietary IoT devices and cloud platform
- **Embedded finance**: Flexible financing tailored to farmers' growth
- **Holistic insurance**: Disease insurance protecting against crop uncertainties
- **Marketplace**: Direct market linkage for better pricing and profitability
- **Ecosystem services**: Connecting farmers directly to input providers (hatcheries, feeds) and buyers

## Key Technology
### Monitoring Products
- **CheckTray**: Smart check tray for feed consumption and shrimp behavior monitoring
- **WaterMon**: GPS-enabled smart handheld device for real-time water quality monitoring
- **Patholense**: AI-powered microscopy for early pathogen detection
- **PumpMon**: Real-time pump performance and water flow monitoring
- **PowerMon/APFC**: Power monitoring + automatic power factor correction

### Automation Products
- **AquaBot**: Automated precision feeding across ponds with app control
- **Static Feeders** (100kg/200kg): App-controlled feeding with high-capacity hoppers
- **Feed Mixer**: Uniform feed blending for additives and probiotics
- **Starter**: Smart aerator controller with remote management
- **Smart Scale**: Real-time weight validation synced to feeder and app
- **FeedMon**: Acoustic demand-based intelligent feeding — feeds only when shrimp demand it (minimizes waste, maximizes FCR)
- **ProBrew Master**: Temperature-controlled probiotic brewing
- **Shrimp Counter**: AI-powered smartphone imaging for stock estimation

### Software
- **NextFarm App**: Comprehensive farm management for data-driven decisions

## Data & Measurement Needs
- **Primary data types**: Water quality parameters (temperature, pH, dissolved oxygen, salinity, ammonia, nitrite), feed consumption data, shrimp behavior patterns, pathogen detection results, pump/power performance telemetry, acoustic feeding demand signals, shrimp biomass/stock estimates
- **Key measurements/parameters**: Dissolved oxygen (mg/L), water temperature (°C), pH, salinity (ppt), ammonia, nitrite, feed conversion ratio (FCR), survival rate, growth rate, disease incidence, power consumption, water flow rates
- **Observation platforms/programs**: IoT sensor networks across 20,000 devices in ponds; AI-powered microscopy for pathogen detection; acoustic monitoring for feeding demand; GPS-enabled water quality monitoring; cloud-based longitudinal farm performance tracking
- **Known data gaps**: Standardized water quality baselines across shrimp farming regions; early-warning disease prediction models; integration with satellite-derived environmental data (temperature, precipitation, chlorophyll); cross-farm benchmarking at scale
- **Interest in external data services**: Satellite-derived pond temperature and water quality proxies; weather forecasting for farm management; regional disease outbreak surveillance data; market price data for marketplace optimization

## Cross-Sector Connections
- **Aquaculture**: AquaExchange extends the aquaculture sector to shrimp farming — complementing Innovasea (sensors/telemetry), Aquabyte (computer vision for salmon), ReelData AI (land-based RAS), Tidal (underwater vision), and BiOceanOr (water quality forecasting). The full-stack IoT approach (hardware + finance + insurance + marketplace) is distinct from single-product competitors
- **Marine Monitoring & Sensors**: WaterMon and Patholense are sensor platforms — overlap with Dottir Labs (Raman spectroscopy water quality), Nucleic Sensing Systems (eDNA monitoring)
- **Acoustic feeding** (FeedMon): Novel approach using acoustic listening for demand-based feeding — distinct from camera-based approaches (Aquabyte, Tidal, NeuralX) and schedule-based automation
