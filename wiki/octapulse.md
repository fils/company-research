# OctaPulse

**Sector**: Aquaculture
**Official Site**: https://www.tryoctapulse.com/
**HQ**: Beacon, NY, USA
**Last Updated**: 2026-05-17

## Overview

OctaPulse develops AI-powered inspection systems and robotics for aquaculture, enabling precision phenotyping, selective breeding, and automated fish hatchery operations. The company combines computer vision, robotics, and data science to transform how fish are farmed — achieving 98%+ accuracy on 100,000+ fish analyzed.

**Tagline:** "Robotics for Aquaculture — Precision phenotyping, selective breeding & robotic automation"

## Business Model

Hardware + software platform. OctaPulse sells robotic inspection and grading systems with an AI software subscription for phenotyping and breeding optimization. Their technology automates juvenile fish grading in hatcheries, reducing labor costs by ~50%.

## Key Technology

- **Computer Vision Phenotyping** — Real-time measurement of fish size, weight, and physical traits
- **Selective Breeding Engine** — Combines phenotypic, genetic, and environmental data for breeding decisions
- **Robotic Automation** — Multi-source computer vision integrated with robotic handling
- **24/7 Autonomous Monitoring** — Continuous operation with 98%+ accuracy

## Data & Measurement Needs

- **Primary data types**: Fish phenotypic data (size, weight, physical traits), genetic data for selective breeding, environmental water quality, feed conversion ratio data, fish health/behavioral imagery
- **Key measurements/parameters**: Fish length, weight, condition factor, feed intake and conversion efficiency, water temperature, dissolved oxygen, pH, stocking density, growth rates, genetic lineage
- **Observation platforms/programs**: In-tank computer vision monitoring (24/7), automated fish inspection during handling/grading, environmental sensor arrays at farm sites
- **Known data gaps**: Lacks real-time water chemistry data at molecular level; genetic data integration from sequencing is manual; farm-level environmental data needs denser sensor coverage
- **Interest in external data services**: High — genetic data, environmental monitoring, and water quality analytics would enhance selective breeding models

## Relevance to Marine Data Markets

OctaPulse's data needs align with the broader aquaculture sector's demand for denser environmental monitoring. Their selective breeding models could benefit from integrated water chemistry data (creating a synergy opportunity with Dottir Labs). Their positioning in Y Combinator and VentureWell suggests strong growth trajectory.
