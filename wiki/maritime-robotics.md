# Maritime Robotics

**Sector**: Marine Monitoring & Sensors
**Official Site**: https://www.maritimerobotics.com/

## Overview

Maritime Robotics is a Norwegian USV (uncrewed surface vessel) and autonomous navigation pioneer, founded in 2005 in Trondheim. The company provides integrated sea drone solutions combining autonomous navigation, vessel design, and modular payload systems for mission-critical dual-use operations (commercial + defense). With hundreds of autonomous systems delivered and revenue growing ~5x over 5 years (2x in last 2 years), the company is positioned at the forefront of the structural shift from pilot projects to fleet-level autonomous maritime deployment.

## Business Model

**Hardware + autonomy platform sales**:
- Turn-key USVs: **Mariner X** (modular sensor platform), **Otter** (smaller USV), and **mine countermeasures USV** (launched Jan 2026)
- **SEACONTROL** — autonomous navigation system that retrofits to both crewed and uncrewed surface vessels
- **Modular payload architecture** — vessels are sensor-agnostic platforms accepting customer-specific payloads
- **Dual-use commercial + defense**: serves industrial (offshore energy, hydrography, environmental monitoring, fisheries management) and defense (mine countermeasures, maritime security) markets
- **Fleet-level deployment strategy**: customers shifting from pilot projects to fleet-level — reflects accelerating structural demand

## Context

Maritime Robotics represents the **structural maturation** of the USV market — the company's quote that "we are now past the experimentation phase" and customers are shifting to "fleet-level deployment strategies" signals a sector-wide transition. This is reinforced by the company's 5x revenue growth over 5 years and the €28M growth investment to scale manufacturing. As a 2005-founded pioneer with hundreds of systems delivered, Maritime Robotics brings a 20-year operational track record that distinguishes it from newer ASV entrants (Saronic, Blue Water Autonomy, HavocAI) that are earlier in commercial deployment.
