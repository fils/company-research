# Principle Power

**Sector**: Offshore Energy
**Official Site**: https://www.principlepower.com/

**Overview**
Global leader in floating offshore wind technology with the WindFloat® platform. Provides full lifecycle engineering services. The WindFloat is one of the most proven floating wind foundation technologies, deployed on multiple commercial-scale projects.

**Technology**
- WindFloat® — semi-submersible floating wind platform
- Full lifecycle engineering services
- Proven in multiple commercial deployments
- Compatible with all major turbine OEMs

**Business Model**
Technology licensing + engineering services for floating offshore wind projects. B2B to wind developers, utilities, and EPC contractors.

### Data & Measurement Needs
- Primary data types: Site metocean characterization, seafloor data, environmental impact baselines
- Key measurements: Wave climate (height/period/direction), current profiles, wind resource, water depth, seafloor sediments, marine ecology
- Observation platforms: Metocean buoys, LIDAR, geotechnical surveys, environmental monitoring campaigns
- Known data gaps: Long-duration site-specific metocean data required for WindFloat design optimization
- Interest in external data services: Critical — platform design optimization, cost reduction, and project financing all depend on high-quality metocean data
