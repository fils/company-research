# Offshore Energy

**Overview (as of May 2026)**

Offshore energy companies in the knowledge graph are primarily focused on floating offshore wind, with supporting marine environmental monitoring, metocean data, and installation/maintenance services.

**Core Themes**
- Floating wind platform technology
- Deep-water wind development
- High demand for oceanographic and meteorological (metocean) data
- Extensive marine environmental surveys and biodiversity monitoring requirements

**Companies Profiled**

- **Ørsted**: World's leading offshore wind developer and operator. Full-cycle EPC capabilities. Very high marine environmental survey and biodiversity monitoring needs.

- **Gazelle Wind Power**: Developer of lightweight, modular floating platforms with dynamic mooring system. Focus on easier deployment for +15 MW turbines. Backed by dstgroup.

- **Principle Power**: Global leader in floating wind technology (WindFloat®). Provides full lifecycle engineering services. Proven track record with 105 MW installed.

- **BW Ideol**: Floating offshore wind platform provider using Damping Pool® technology. 12+ years experience. Recently secured major factory funding (€126M) and partnerships (Holcim).

**Key Insights**
- All floating wind companies require high-quality metocean data and marine environmental baselines.
- Installation, IMR (Inspect, Maintain, Repair), and decommissioning phases represent major data and service needs.
- Strong synergy with climate risk providers for physical risk modeling of assets.
