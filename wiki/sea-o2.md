**Business Model**: Electrochemical Direct Ocean Capture (DOC) for CDR credits and Blue CO₂ utilization (green CO₂ feedstock). Sell high-quality credits to neutralize emissions; sell captured CO₂ for industrial use (methanol, food, packaging); storage services and partnerships. Renewable-powered, scalable with low volume needs compared to DAC.

**Key Technology**: Uses seawater and renewable electricity + electrochemistry/vacuum to extract gaseous CO₂ from dissolved inorganic carbon (DIC) in surface seawater. Decarbonized water returned to surface to draw down atmospheric CO₂. No added chemicals. Fights ocean acidification locally.

**Funding & Investors**: Commercialized; EU BlueInvest recognition, EU project participation. Early revenue via pilots/partners.

**Key Insights 2026**: Pilot in North Sea (2025) validated; 2026 pilots nameplate 25 tCO₂/yr each with standalone operation planned in South Holland. Vision for gigaton-scale. Focus on MRV+ocean health.

**Data & Measurement Needs**
- Primary data types: Ocean carbonate chemistry (DIC removal quantification), pH changes, alkalinity shifts.
- Key measurements/parameters: Dissolved Inorganic Carbon (DIC), pH, total alkalinity (TA), salinity, temperature, dissolved oxygen, CO₂ partial pressure (pCO2), ocean acidification metrics.
- Observation platforms/programs: Real-time sensor networks + MRV telemetry (intensive), autonomous underwater vehicle (AUV)/ROV surveys, fixed moorings/towers, baseline ecosystem surveys before/after, satellite validation for spatial coverage.
- Known data gaps: High-resolution, real-time vertical profiles and near-surface DIC/pH/alkalinity data for ongoing verification; long-term ecosystem impact baselines post-deployment; spatially distributed metocean (current, waves) data for multi-site scaling and permitting.
- Interest in external data services: Yes, marine sensor networks, real-time MRV systems, ocean observing data providers, validation campaigns.
