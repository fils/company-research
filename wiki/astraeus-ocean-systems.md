## Astraeus Ocean Systems

**Overview**: Astraeus Ocean Systems (Bellingham, WA) provides direct access to a smart network of autonomous vessels and a comprehensive mariculture management platform. Their "Mariculture Almanac" combines Watchline sensor hardware, Digital Oyster/Digital Kelp biological models, and the Almanac farm management platform.

**Funding**: VentureWell Ocean Enterprise Accelerator Stage 2 ($50K TDC award, Spring 2026). Winner of Bellingham Angel Investors pitch competition (2024).

**Key Technology**:
- **Watchline™ Hardware**: In-situ sensors measuring temperature, salinity, dissolved oxygen, and proprietary optical factors. GPS infrastructure monitoring. Deployed at farm lease sites.
- **Digital Oyster™ & Digital Kelp™**: Proprietary biological state models of shellfish and seaweed physiology. Continuously updated from seeding through harvest.
- **The Almanac Platform**: Tracks stock, infrastructure, and water conditions. Forecasts crop development. Simulates management decisions. Offline-capable mobile app.
- **Astraeus Mission Planner**: Coordinates autonomous surface vessel fleet for wide-area ocean intelligence.

**Business Model**: Subscription-based (annual per farm/site). Hardware + software + support included.

**Data & Measurement Needs**:
- Primary data types: In-situ water quality sensor streams (temperature, salinity, DO, optical), GPS/infrastructure position monitoring, crop physiological data (oyster/kelp growth, condition, stress), metocean forecasts
- Key measurements/parameters: Temperature, salinity, DO, chlorophyll-a (Chl-a), Na⁺, NO₃⁻, Ca²⁺, proprietary optical factors, current speed/direction
- Observation platforms/programs: Continuous Watchline sensor network at farm lease sites (24/7), autonomous surface vessel fleet, GPS-based offshore infrastructure monitoring, crop model continuous updating
- Known data gaps: Insufficient site-specific water quality at farm locations (regional buoys not farm-specific), limited real-time shellfish physiology data for model validation, gap between broad-scale ocean observations and lease-scale monitoring
- Interest in external data services: Very high — core business depends on accurate, continuous environmental data

**Cross-sector relevance**: Bridges aquaculture and ocean intelligence. Similar to **Oshen** (autonomous ocean robots) but focused on mariculture applications. Shellfish/seaweed focus complements **Kelp Blue** (giant kelp cultivation) and a growing US mariculture market.
