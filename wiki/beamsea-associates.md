# BeamSea Associates

**Sector**: Marine Monitoring & Sensors
**Official Site**: https://www.beamsea.com/
**Last Updated**: 2026-05-24

## Business Model
BeamSea Associates automates species-level coral reef ecosystem monitoring at operationally relevant scales using a fluorescence-enhanced, high-resolution 3D LiDAR system. Provides defensible reef health data for conservation and research applications.

## Funding
- **VentureWell/NOAA OEA Stage 1** (Spring 2026) — $15K TDC award
- SBIR/STTR awards (NASA, NOAA)
- Location: Loxahatchee, FL

## Key Technology
- Fluorescence-enhanced 3D LiDAR for underwater biological substrates
- Species-level coral reef bottom cover classification
- Machine learning integration for benthic organism identification
- PLLS-360° underwater LiDAR imaging system
- Non-acoustic underwater sensing alternative

## Data & Measurement Needs
- Primary data types: 3D LiDAR point clouds, multispectral fluorescence returns, benthic community imagery, reef structural metrics
- Key measurements/parameters: Coral species identification, reef bottom cover %, reef structural complexity indices, water turbidity, depth profiles
- Observation platforms/programs: Fixed underwater LiDAR deployments, ROV-towed sensors, reef survey campaigns
- Known data gaps: High-resolution reef species monitoring at scale; real-time underwater data processing; integrating LiDAR with satellite reef observations
- Interest in external data services: Satellite reef health indices (NOAA CRW), historical reef survey baselines, water quality station networks, marine protected area boundary data
