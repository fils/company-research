# Kelp Blue

**Sector**: Ocean Carbon Sequestration
**Official Site**: https://www.kelp.blue/
**Last Updated**: 2026-05-03

**Overview**
Kelp Blue cultivates Giant Kelp (*Macrocystis pyrifera*) on large-scale offshore structures for ocean re-wilding, carbon sequestration, biodiversity enhancement, and production of the StimBlue+ biostimulant for sustainable agriculture. Zero-input offshore farming with 7-year sustainable canopy harvest cycles.

**Business Model**
- Blue carbon sequestration credits from large-scale kelp forests.
- Commercial sale of natural, carbon-neutral biostimulants to regenerative agriculture and high-value crop markets.
- Potential 0.2–0.5 Gt CO₂/yr sequestration by 2050.
- Field trials across Europe, South Africa, Americas with progressive farmer network.

**Key Technology**
- Offshore kelp cultivation on proprietary structures.
- Patented cell-wall bursting for high-quality biostimulant extraction.
- Zero freshwater, land, pesticide, fertilizer use.
- Monitoring of carbon export, biodiversity, and fish stock enhancement.

### Data & Measurement Needs
- **Primary data types**: Oceanographic metocean data, biomass and growth monitoring, satellite kelp canopy mapping, carbon flux/export measurements.
- **Key measurements/parameters**: Kelp biomass stocks and growth rates, CO₂ sequestration rates, nutrient levels, current/wave data, benthic community metrics, fish population changes, alkalinity/pH for acidification reduction.
- **Observation platforms/programs**: Autonomous underwater vehicles (AUV), satellite remote sensing, fixed sensor arrays on cultivation structures, annual marine biodiversity surveys.
- **Known data gaps**: Long-term deep-ocean carbon export measurements, high-resolution spatial kelp forest mapping at commercial scale, quantitative MRV for blue carbon credits.

**Marine Data Pain Points**
Large-scale offshore operations require comprehensive metocean, biomass telemetry, and ecosystem impact data for licensing, credit integrity, and optimization of site performance.

**Contacts / Notes**
Family-run ethos with global operations. Ambassador farmer network for real-world validation. Heavy focus on UN SDGs: jobs, biodiversity, carbon sequestration.
