# Ocean Carbon Sequestration

**Overview (as of May 2026)**

Ocean-based Carbon Dioxide Removal (mCDR) includes several pathways actively represented in the knowledge graph:

- Ocean Alkalinity Enhancement (OAE) – Planetary Technologies, Equatic, Ebb Carbon, Running Tide
- Direct Ocean Capture (DOC) / electrodialysis – Captura, Equatic
- Biomass sinking / macroalgae – Running Tide, Vaulted Deep (BiCRS waste pathway)
- Shipping-based carbon capture – Seabound

**Companies Profiled**

- **Ebb Carbon**: Electrochemical CDR from desalination brine. Converts waste brine into freshwater + acid/caustic + ocean alkalinity. Strong ecological safety focus (PNNL, Microsoft, Stripe, Isometric partners).

- **Captura**: Direct Ocean Capture via high-performance bipolar membrane electrodialysis. 1,000 t/year pilot in Hawaii. Backed by Caltech, Frontier, Stripe, Equinor. Dual-use technology for industrial applications.

- **Planetary Technologies**: Coastal OAE using purified alkaline minerals introduced into existing infrastructure outfalls. Strong academic partnerships (Dalhousie, National Oceanography Centre). Multiple Nova Scotia and US East Coast projects.

- **Running Tide**: Combines OAE (alkaline mineral dissolution) and biomass sinking. 25,000+ tonnes removed, 546 ocean sensors deployed. Customers include Microsoft, Shopify, Stripe. Strong marine data collection focus.

- **Equatic**: Seawater electrolysis producing permanent bicarbonate + mineral carbonates + green hydrogen. $11.6M Series A (2025). Earthshot finalist. First North American commercial-scale facility in planning.

- **Vaulted Deep**: Waste-based BiCRS (Biomass with Carbon Removal and Storage). Injects organic waste deep underground for permanent sequestration. 44,000+ tonnes removed. Major credits sold to Stripe, Meta, Google, Microsoft.

- **Seabound**: Onboard carbon capture retrofitted to cargo ships (25–95% capture rate). Carbonate offloaded at ports. Backed by Y Combinator and Lowercarbon Capital. Strong marine logistics and port integration component.

**Key Insights**
- All pathways have high demand for high-resolution, real-time marine environmental data (carbonate chemistry, pH, DIC, alkalinity, metocean).
- Strong common need for MRV (measurement, reporting, verification) platforms and ecological safety monitoring.
- Multiple companies are moving toward commercial-scale deployments in 2025–2026.
