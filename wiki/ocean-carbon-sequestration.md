# Ocean Carbon Sequestration

**Overview (as of May 2026)**

Ocean-based Carbon Dioxide Removal (mCDR or ocean CDR) includes:

- Ocean Alkalinity Enhancement (OAE) – Planetary Technologies, Equatic, Ebb Carbon
- Direct Ocean Capture (DOC – electrodialysis) – Captura
- Biomass sinking / macroalgae – Running Tide, Carbon Kapture

**Key Companies Profiled**
- Ebb Carbon → brine electrochemical, desalination synergies, freshwater + CDR
- Captura → DOC electrodialysis, Hawaii pilot + industrial platform, strong $ backing
- Planetary Technologies → OAE via coastal outfalls, academic heavy, Nova Scotia projects
- Running Tide → biomass sinking + OAE, 25k+ tonnes credited, high sensor density MRV
- Equatic → seawater electrolysis, bicarbonate + green H2, $11.6M Series A 2025, first NA commercial facility

**Emerging Needs & Insights**
Marine data demand is universally high across companies:
- Continuous carbonate chemistry telemetry (pH, DIC, alkalinity)
- High-resolution dispersion & mixing models
- Biological ecosystem baseline + impact monitoring
- Real-time sensor networks & MRV traceability (dozens to hundreds of sensors per project)

**Funding Snapshot**
Captura, Equatic, Running Tide have raised institutional rounds (Equatic $11.6M Series A, Captura multiple rounds with Equinor/Aramco).

**Cross-sector Connections**
All companies need:
- Oceanographic hindcasts and Lagrangian transport models
- MRV frameworks compliant with Oxford Principles / Isometric / Stripe standards
- Heavy reliance on NOAA, academic oceanography labs, and private sensor networks
