# Gazelle Wind Power

**Sector**: Offshore Energy
**Official Site**: https://gazellewindpower.com/

**Overview**
Gazelle Wind Power develops lightweight, modular floating offshore wind platforms with a dynamic mooring system. Focus on reducing LCOE through simplified design, port-side assembly, and reduced steel requirements.

**Technology**
- Lightweight modular floating platform design
- Dynamic mooring system for deep-water deployment
- Port-side assembly reducing offshore installation costs
- Reduced steel content compared to conventional floaters

**Business Model**
Technology licensing + project development partnerships. B2B sales to offshore wind developers and utilities.

### Data & Measurement Needs
- Primary data types: Metocean data, seafloor characterization, environmental impact data
- Key measurements: Wave height/period, current profiles, wind speed, water depth, seafloor geology, marine mammal surveys
- Observation platforms: Metocean buoys, geotechnical surveys, environmental baseline studies
- Known data gaps: High-resolution site-specific metocean data for platform design optimization
- Interest in external data services: High — platform design and deployment location decisions depend on comprehensive metocean data
