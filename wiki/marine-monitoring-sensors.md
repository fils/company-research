# Marine Monitoring & Sensors

**Last Updated**: 2026-05-24  
**Companies Tracked**: 4

This sector covers companies building coral reef monitoring, autonomous surface vessels (ASVs), autonomous underwater vehicles (AUVs), and other marine sensor platforms. These companies provide the observational infrastructure that enables data-driven ocean management, conservation, and scientific research.

## Companies

### BeamSea Associates
Automated species-level coral reef ecosystem monitoring using fluorescence-enhanced 3D LiDAR + ML. SBIR/STTR funded (NASA, NOAA).
- **Site**: [beamsea.com](https://www.beamsea.com/)
- **Funding**: VentureWell/NOAA OEA Stage 1 (Spring 2026) — $15K TDC award
- **Location**: Loxahatchee, FL

### AquaFrontier Technologies
Biomimetic autonomous hardware + cloud data pipelines to democratize ocean observation. Making real-time ocean intelligence accessible beyond funded research institutions.
- **Site**: [aquafrontier.tech](https://aquafrontier.tech/)
- **Funding**: VentureWell/NOAA OEA Stage 0 (Spring 2026)
- **Location**: Brooklyn, NY

### Omission Inc
Portable autonomous surface vessels (ASVs) that reduce nearshore survey costs by 50-70%. Also builds UGVs. Serves defense, research, and marine industries.
- **Site**: [omissioninc.com](https://www.omissioninc.com/)
- **Funding**: VentureWell/NOAA OEA Stage 0 (Spring 2026)
- **Location**: Portland, ME

## Cross-Company Marine Data Patterns
- **Autonomous platforms**: All three companies build autonomous systems (ASVs, AUVs) for persistent ocean monitoring
- **Survey-grade data**: High-resolution oceanographic and environmental data collection from autonomous platforms
- **Cost reduction**: Common theme of reducing marine data collection costs through automation (Omission 50-70%, AquaFrontier "democratizing")
- **Conservation focus**: BeamSea targets coral reef health; AquaFrontier and Omission serve broader ocean observation needs
- **Hardware + data**: All three combine physical autonomous platforms with data delivery (cloud dashboards, processed datasets)

## Data & Measurement Needs
Marine monitoring companies need:
- Oceanographic sensor data (temperature, salinity, dissolved oxygen, pH)
- Bathymetric and seafloor mapping data
- Underwater acoustic data
- Water quality station network data
- Satellite remote sensing validation data (SST, chlorophyll, sea surface height)
- Coastal baseline survey data
