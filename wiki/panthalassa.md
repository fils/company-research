# Panthalassa

**Sector**: Offshore Energy
**Official Site**: https://panthalassa.com/

**Overview**
Panthalassa is building a planetary-scale clean energy platform in the middle of the ocean, using wave energy to power AI computing and data centers at sea. Public-benefit corporation with team from SpaceX, Google, Blue Origin, NASA. Ocean-2 pilot deployment underway in northern Pacific.

**Technology**
- Wave energy conversion platform integrated with offshore compute nodes
- Ocean-2 pilot series in northern Pacific
- Scalable offshore infrastructure for AI data centers
- Zero-emission power generation

**Business Model**
Clean energy generation + offshore AI compute services. Revenue from energy sales, compute services, and renewable energy certificates.

**Recent Highlights**
- $140M Series B (May 2026) led by Peter Thiel
- Also backed by John Doerr, Marc Benioff's TIME Ventures, Max Levchin's SciFi VC
- Lowercarbon Capital (early backer)
- Valuation ~$1B (unicorn status)
- Team from SpaceX, Google, Blue Origin, NASA, Tesla, Apple

### Data & Measurement Needs
- Primary data types: Wave resource assessment, metocean data, seafloor surveys, environmental impact data
- Key measurements: Wave height/frequency/direction, currents, wind, water depth, seafloor conditions, seawater temperature
- Observation platforms: Metocean buoys, satellite data, AUV/ROV surveys, weather station networks
- Known data gaps: High-resolution wave energy maps in deployment regions; long-term metocean time series at proposed sites
- Interest in external data services: Critical — site selection, platform engineering, permitting all require extensive metocean data
