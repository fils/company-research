# Vycarb

**Sector**: Aquaculture (with strong Carbon Removal overlap)

**Official Site**: https://vycarb.com/

**Overview**  
Vycarb develops sensor-driven carbon capture and storage systems that work in any water body. It combines novel carbon neutralization chemistry with proprietary real-time carbon sensing technology for measurable, permanent CO₂ removal.

**Technology**  
- Passive system using natural water flow  
- Converts CO₂ into stable bicarbonate (HCO₃)  
- Real-time sensing for precise, verifiable quantification  
- Autonomous operation with no manual intervention

**Business Model**  
- Technology solution provider (not project developer)  
- Sells hardware + sensing platform that enables others to deploy carbon-negative solutions  
- $5M seed (Oct 2025, Twynam lead; Shell, Hatch Blue, Singapore govt)
- Partners: Tomco Systems (industrial CO₂ management — accepts point-source CO₂ gas as input), atdepth (MIT spinout, Google/NASA-backed ocean monitoring for independent MRV verification)
- Industrial gas integration: converts industrial CO₂ to bicarbonate for water storage; handles low-purity CO₂
- Clients include Klarna, Frontier, U.S. DOE, Stripe; pipeline in US, Europe, Asia Pacific
- Founded 2022 by CEO Garrett Boudinot; Brooklyn-based

**Marine Relevance**  
Very high. Directly operates in marine and freshwater environments with strong requirements for accurate carbon chemistry sensing and environmental monitoring.

**Recent Progress**  
- East River NYC pilot running 1+ year with independent atdepth monitoring
- Moving from pilots to commercial deployments in 2026
- Expanding internationally (US, Europe, Asia Pacific)
- Industrial gas integration partnership with Tomco Systems for point-source CO₂
