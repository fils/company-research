## Overview

pHathom Technologies (Atlantic Canada) develops a modular, infrastructure-light carbon removal solution that bolts directly onto existing coastal biomass power plants. The technology captures emitted CO₂, dissolves it in seawater, and uses limestone to convert it into stable calcium bicarbonate — accelerating the natural mineral weathering process that stores carbon in the ocean for millennia (>10,000 years).

The process provides two powerful outcomes simultaneously:
1. Durably remove and store atmospheric CO₂ as ocean bicarbonate.
2. Counter ocean acidification in local coastal waters, supporting marine life (shell and coral formation).

**Key Value Proposition**: Scalable, cost-effective, measurable, low-energy BECCS-ocean hybrid CDR with explicit marine health co-benefits. Commercialization target 2030; potential to reach gigaton-scale annual removal.

## Key Technology

**Process Flow**:
1. Capture CO₂ from biomass combustion flue gas.
2. Convert: slurry of limestone + seawater reacts with CO₂ to form bicarbonate.
3. Discharge: stable bicarbonate released into the ocean — permanently sequestered and pH-neutral.

**Competitive Advantages**:
- Extremely low energy intensity.
- No pipelines, wells, or deep geologic storage required.
- Modular and rapidly deployable.
- Measurable end-to-end via on-site pH/alkalinity + downstream aquatic monitoring.
- Generates ocean health co-benefits (addresses calcium bicarbonate shortage caused by acidification).
