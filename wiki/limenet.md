# Limenet

**Sector**: Ocean Carbon Sequestration
**Official Site**: https://limenet.tech/
**Last Updated**: 2026-05-03

**Overview**
Limenet develops industrial-scale technology to convert CO₂ into stable calcium bicarbonate solutions for permanent storage in the ocean while indirectly counteracting local ocean acidification. Their patented "pH Equilibrated Ocean Alkalinization" process uses carbon-free lime produced from calcium carbonate + seawater + renewable energy in a controlled reactor, producing a transparent solution with seawater-like pH.

**Business Model**
Sales of verified, permanent carbon removal credits (third-party ISO 14064-2 certified). Buyers include Stripe, Shopify, KlimaDAO, Frontier Climate, Google. Industrial pilot plants (LimePlant) with gigaton-scale modular scalability potential. EU-backed (NextGenerationEU) + investors Moonstone, Axel Carbon, CDP.

**Key Technology**
- Patented LimePlant for production of carbon-free lime / calcium bicarbonate.
- Reactor-based controlled dissolution (avoids turbidity and pH spikes of traditional OAE).
- Direct injection of equilibrated alkaline solution.
- Plants in La Spezia (tens tCO₂/yr → 800 tCO₂/yr), Augusta (Sicily) scaling to 1,500 tons/yr lime capacity.

### Data & Measurement Needs
- **Primary data types**: Marine carbonate chemistry monitoring pre/post-injection, alkalinity budgets, pH stability tracking.
- **Key measurements/parameters**: pH, total alkalinity, DIC, calcium, temperature, salinity; long-term (10k–100k year) stability via modeling.
- **Observation platforms/programs**: Controlled reactor output validation, pilot site marine surveys, third-party MRV certification campaigns.
- **Known data gaps**: Long-term ecosystem response to increased bicarbonate buffering at scale; high-resolution spatial alkalinity mapping around injection points.

**Marine Data Pain Points**
Precise, real-time carbonate system data at injection and reference sites is critical for MRV and demonstrating acidification mitigation benefits.

**Contacts / Notes**
Italian benefit company. Strong scientific validation history (Lake Orta liming analog). Focus on natural-process acceleration with minimal local disruption.
