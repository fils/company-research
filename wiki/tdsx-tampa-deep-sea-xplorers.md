# TDSX (Tampa Deep Sea Xplorers)

**Sector**: Marine Monitoring & Sensors
**Official Site**: https://tampadeepseaxplorers.com/

## Overview
TDSX radically improves cost-effectiveness of underwater exploration and data collection through its Barracuda AUV — an autonomous underwater vehicle designed for deeper dives, greater maneuverability, and faster data collection at lower cost. Actively commercializing (orders open).

## Business Model
- **Product:** Barracuda AUV — autonomous underwater vehicle for ocean data
- **Markets:** Offshore energy (O&G, wind, wave), oceanographic research, military & security
- **Stage:** Early commercialization (accepting orders)
- **Funding:** NOAA SBIR, DoD SBIR, DHS S&T SBIR, equity crowdfunding (StartEngine); VentureWell/NOAA OEA Stage 0 (Spring 2026)

## Key Technology
- **Barracuda AUV:** Small-footprint autonomous underwater vehicle
- **Capabilities:** Sub-bottom profiling, water column characterization, ocean current profiles, tides, temperature, salinity, water clarity, bio-luminescence measurement
- **Differentiator:** Radical cost reduction + superior performance specs vs. incumbent AUVs

### Data & Measurement Needs
- **Primary data types:** Water column profiles, sub-bottom profiles, ocean current data, bathymetry
- **Key measurements/parameters:** Temperature, salinity, current speed/direction, tide, water clarity, bio-luminescence, ocean pollution
- **Observation platforms:** Barracuda AUV (primary sensor delivery platform)
- **Known data gaps:** Deep-sea real-time telemetry; affordable ocean observation for small commercial operators and academic institutions
- **Interest in external data services:** NOAA data integration; partnerships with oceanographic research institutions; integration with satellite ocean data for cross-validation

## Key Partnerships
- NOAA SBIR, DoD (USSOCOM), DHS S&T, USF Mechanical Engineering, Braid Theory (marine tech incubator), Hello Tomorrow (Deep Tech Pioneers), XPRISE Ocean Discovery
