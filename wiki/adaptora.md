# Adaptora

**Sector**: Coastal Risk & Infrastructure
**Official Site**: https://www.tensora.tech/
**Last Updated**: 2026-05-24

## Business Model
Adaptora (a product of Tensora Inc.) is developing a subsurface risk intelligence platform that combines depth-resolved monitoring to help coastal decision-makers mitigate land subsidence risks. They provide ultra-high resolution ground deformation sensing to detect and predict coastal land sinking, a critical but often overlooked climate risk factor.

## Funding
- **VentureWell/NOAA OEA Stage 1** (Spring 2026) — $15K TDC award
- Parent company: Tensora Inc.

## Key Technology
- **Ultra-high resolution ground deformation sensors**: Precision subsidence measurement
- **Depth-resolved monitoring**: Multi-layer subsurface data collection
- **Subsurface risk intelligence platform**: Decision-support tools for coastal resilience planning
- **Governors Island deployment**: Pilot sensor installation for NYC coastal monitoring

## Data & Measurement Needs
- **Primary data types**: Ground deformation sensor data, subsurface stratigraphy data, coastal elevation surveys, hydrological measurements, satellite InSAR data
- **Key measurements/parameters**: Land subsidence rate (mm/yr), groundwater level, soil moisture content, sediment compaction, coastal elevation change, tidal influence on groundwater
- **Observation platforms/programs**: Ultra-high resolution ground deformation sensors, coastal survey campaigns, satellite InSAR (Sentinel-1)
- **Known data gaps**: Lack of real-time subsidence monitoring across broad coastal regions; integration challenges with existing coastal resilience data platforms; limited public access to subsurface geotechnical data
- **Interest in external data services**: NOAA tide gauge and sea level data, USGS subsurface monitoring wells, InSAR satellite subsidence products, municipal water extraction records, NYC coastal resilience project data
