# Orca AI

**Sector**: Maritime Operations & Analytics
**Official Site**: https://www.orca-ai.io/
**Last Updated**: 2026-07-13

**Focus**: AI-powered computer vision and situational awareness for maritime navigation safety, collision avoidance, and autonomous shipping.
**Status**: 1,500+ vessels deployed; $111M total funding; market leader in maritime AI navigation.
**Founded**: 2018, Tel Aviv, Israel — Yarden Gross (CEO) & Dor Raviv (CTO)

## Business Model
SaaS platform + hardware (SeaPod sensor unit). Per-vessel hardware installation + recurring software subscription for FleetView office-side analytics. Dual-revenue model expanding into defense & government sectors. Customers include MSC, NYK, Scorpio, Seaspan, Maran Tankers, North Standard.

## Funding
- **Series B**: $72.5M (May 2025) — Brighton Park Capital (lead), Ankona Capital, Hyperlink Ventures, OCV Partners, Mizmaa Ventures
- **Total**: $111M over 5 rounds (first round Jan 2019)

## Key Technology
- World's largest marine visual dataset (80M+ nautical miles of data)
- AI-powered computer vision for real-time target detection, classification, and alerting
- Near-zero visibility navigation in congested waters
- GPS spoofing detection
- Co-Captain autonomous navigation assistant
- Lloyd's Register independently assessed technology in live vessel trial (Apr 2026)

## Data & Measurement Needs

- **Primary data types**: Real-time visual/sensor streams from bridge-mounted cameras (day/night/thermal), AIS data, vessel telemetry, GPS positioning, satellite connectivity (Starlink-enabled)
- **Key measurements/parameters**: Target distance (CPA/TCPA), vessel heading, speed, bearing, visibility conditions, sea state, traffic density, fuel consumption
- **Observation platforms/programs**: Ship-mounted optical + thermal sensor network (1,500+ vessels), fleet-wide continuous data collection (120M+ nautical miles), satellite-augmented real-time data transmission
- **Known data gaps**: Limited coverage in polar regions and deep ocean transit zones; relies on vessel density for network effect data collection; no subsurface oceanographic data
- **Interest in external data services**: Satellite AIS augmentation, weather routing integration, ocean current data for voyage optimization, metocean forecasts for route planning
