# ORCA

**Sector**: Aquaculture  
**Official Site**: https://www.orca-ocean.ai/  
**Last Updated**: 2026-05-24

## Business Model
ORCA is pioneering AI foundation modeling of the oceans to predict environmental shocks to fisheries and aquaculture operators. Their technology applies large-scale AI models trained on oceanographic data to forecast conditions like harmful algal blooms, temperature anomalies, and oxygen depletion events that threaten marine food production.

## Key Technology
- **AI Foundation Models**: Large-scale models trained on oceanographic datasets for environmental shock prediction
- **Fisheries Forecasting**: Predictive analytics for conditions threatening wild fisheries
- **Aquaculture Early Warning**: Alerts for harmful algal blooms, temperature spikes, dissolved oxygen drops
- **Ocean Intelligence Platform**: Real-time environmental risk assessment for marine food producers

## Data & Measurement Needs
- **Primary data types**: Oceanographic time-series data, satellite ocean imagery, fisheries catch data, aquaculture production metrics, harmful algal bloom monitoring data
- **Key measurements/parameters**: Sea surface temperature, chlorophyll-a concentration, dissolved oxygen levels, pH, nutrient concentrations (nitrate, phosphate), harmful algal toxin levels, fish biomass
- **Observation platforms/programs**: Satellite ocean monitoring (SST, chlorophyll), in-situ buoy networks, fisheries observer programs, aquaculture farm sensors
- **Known data gaps**: Limited high-frequency oceanographic data in coastal fishing zones; insufficient historical baseline data for rare shock events; sparse integration of fisheries operational data with oceanographic forecasting
- **Interest in external data services**: NOAA oceanographic buoy data feeds, satellite chlorophyll/SST products, state fisheries monitoring data, real-time water quality station networks, climate model forecast outputs
