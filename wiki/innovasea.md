# Innovasea

**Sector**: Aquaculture
**Official Site**: https://www.innovasea.com/

## Overview
Global leader in sustainable aquaculture and fish tracking technology. Provides real-time environmental sensors (aquaMeasure), acoustic telemetry (NexTrak), and cloud-based aquaculture management software (Fathom).

## Business Model
- Sustainable aquaculture technology (submersible net pens, precision aquaculture)
- Fish tracking solutions for research (acoustic telemetry)
- Cloud-based data management (Fathom software, aquaHub)
- AI fish counting (HydroAI)
- Global presence: US, Chile (Puerto Varas facility)

## Products
- **aquaMeasure**: Real-time environmental sensors (O2, salinity, temp, depth, algae)
- **NexTrak**: Advanced acoustic telemetry for fish tracking
- **Fathom**: Integrated aquaculture data management platform
- **Submersible Net Pens**: Open-ocean farming in harsh environments
- **HydroAI**: AI-powered fish counting

## Data & Measurement Needs
- Primary data types: Real-time sensor streams, acoustic telemetry data, underwater camera feeds, environmental monitoring
- Key measurements: Temperature, dissolved oxygen, salinity, depth, fish position, biomass, water quality indices, algae levels
- Observation platforms: Wireless aquaMeasure sensors, acoustic receivers, underwater cameras, cloud communications (aquaHub)
- Known data gaps: Harsh open-ocean sensor deployment, real-time data in high-noise environments, AI fish counting accuracy
- Interest in external data services: Critical — entire business depends on marine sensor data infrastructure
