# Boxfish Robotics

**Sector**: Marine Monitoring & Sensors
**Official Site**: https://www.boxfishrobotics.com/

## Overview
Boxfish Robotics (Auckland, NZ) builds hovering AUVs, ROVs, and resident autonomous vehicles (ARV-i) for marine science, coral reef/benthic monitoring, aquaculture, infrastructure inspection, and underwater filming. 11-50 employees. AI-powered (NVIDIA Jetson, ROS2), 6DOF precision navigation. Active demos at ICRS 2026. Strong customer base in research and conservation.

## Business Model
- Hardware sales (AUV/ROV/ARV + photogrammetry accessories)
- Custom solutions, training, support for research/monitoring/aquaculture ops
- Persistent resident vehicles for long-term autonomous data collection without human presence

## Funding / Stage
- Product revenue + growth stage; BlueSwell participant

## Key Technology
- Boxfish AUV: tetherless hovering, 600m, AI autonomy for repeatable reef/benthic surveys
- ARV-i: resident AUV for aquaculture/infra
- High-res video, 3D photogrammetry, environmental sensors

### Data & Measurement Needs
- **Primary data types:** Imagery/video, 3D models, sensor data, biodiversity/infra assessments
- **Key measurements/parameters:** Temp/salinity/DO/currents, species, structural degradation, fish health
- **Observation platforms/programs:** Autonomous reef monitoring (ICRS), aquaculture farms, ports
- **Known data gaps:** Scalable persistent monitoring + AI classification at operational scale
- **Interest in external data services:** Real-time ocean data, satellite fusion, MRV networks, AI datasets
