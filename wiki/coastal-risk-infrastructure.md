# Coastal Risk & Infrastructure

**Last Updated**: 2026-05-25
**Companies Tracked**: 3

This sector covers companies building subsurface risk intelligence, structural monitoring, and infrastructure resilience platforms for coastal areas — critical for climate adaptation planning in areas vulnerable to land subsidence, sea level rise, structural degradation, and coastal flooding.

## Companies

### Adaptora (Tensora Inc.)
Subsurface risk intelligence platform with depth-resolved monitoring to mitigate land subsidence risks. Ultra-high resolution ground deformation sensors. Deploying on Governors Island, NYC.
- **Site**: [tensora.tech](https://www.tensora.tech/) (parent: Tensora Inc.)
- **Funding**: VentureWell/NOAA OEA Stage 1 (Spring 2026) — $15K TDC award

### Mira Intel (NEW)
AI drone-based infrastructure condition assessment for ports, maritime, energy/utilities. Active projects at Governor's Island and Brooklyn Army Terminal, NY. Boston, MA.
- **Site**: [miraintel.com](https://miraintel.com/)
- **Funding**: VentureWell/NOAA OEA Stage 2 (Spring 2026) — $50K TDC award

### Polaris EcoSystems (NEW)
Photogrammetric 3D reconstruction for automated remote monitoring of structural degradation in piers, ports, shoreline assets. Time-indexable 3D reconstructions. Austin, TX.
- **Site**: [labs.polariseco.com](https://labs.polariseco.com/)
- **Funding**: VentureWell/NOAA OEA Stage 1 (Spring 2026) — $15K TDC award

## Cross-Company Marine Data Patterns
- **Subsurface monitoring**: Ground deformation sensors (Adaptora) provide critical data for coastal resilience planning
- **Aerial condition assessment**: AI drone imagery (Mira Intel) bridges the above-water structural monitoring gap — ports, piers, coastal assets
- **Photogrammetric tracking**: Time-indexable 3D reconstructions (Polaris EcoSystems) enable quantified decay tracking for proactive maintenance
- **Integration needs**: Combining subsurface + aerial + photogrammetric data with tidal records, sea level rise projections, and municipal infrastructure planning
- **Climate adaptation interface**: These companies bridge ocean monitoring and city planning — often overlooked in standard marine data pipelines
- **Full-stack infrastructure monitoring**: Together these three cover below-ground (Adaptora), above-water (Mira Intel), and 3D photogrammetric (Polaris) monitoring of coastal infrastructure

## Data & Measurement Needs
Coastal risk companies need:
- Ground deformation measurements (InSAR, GPS, tiltmeters)
- Tidal gauge and sea level data (NOAA)
- Subsurface hydrology (groundwater, sediment compaction)
- Municipal infrastructure planning data
- Coastal erosion and flooding baseline surveys
- Aerial/satellite imagery for structural assessment
- 3D point cloud data for photogrammetric reconstruction
- Long-term structural degradation baselines in marine environments
- Coastal climate projections and sea-level rise scenarios
