# Coastal Risk & Infrastructure

**Last Updated**: 2026-05-24  
**Companies Tracked**: 1

This sector covers companies building subsurface risk intelligence and ground deformation monitoring platforms for coastal resilience — critical for climate adaptation planning in areas vulnerable to land subsidence, sea level rise, and coastal flooding.

## Cross-Company Marine Data Patterns
- **Subsurface monitoring**: Ground deformation sensors provide critical data for coastal resilience planning
- **Integration needs**: Combining subsurface data with tidal records, sea level rise projections, and municipal infrastructure planning
- **Climate adaptation interface**: These companies bridge ocean monitoring and city planning — often overlooked in standard marine data pipelines

## Data & Measurement Needs
Coastal risk companies need:
- Ground deformation measurements (InSAR, GPS, tiltmeters)
- Tidal gauge and sea level data (NOAA)
- Subsurface hydrology (groundwater, sediment compaction)
- Municipal infrastructure planning data
- Coastal erosion and flooding baseline surveys
