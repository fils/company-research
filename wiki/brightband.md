## Brightband

**Overview**: Brightband is building an end-to-end AI Earth System for probabilistic weather and climate forecasting. Founded by ex-Google X executive Julian Green, the company open-sources benchmark datasets, models, and metrics to establish a common task for global weather forecasting.

**Funding**: $10M Series A (2024) led by Prelude Ventures, with Starshot Capital and CLAI Ventures.

**Key Technology**:
- End-to-end AI Earth System for probabilistic forecasting
- NNJA-AI dataset: AI-ready version of NOAA/NASA Joint Archive
- Open-source weather/climate benchmarks
- Tools for academia, government, and industry

**Team**: Julian Green (CEO, ex-Google X), Daniel Rothenberg (Co-Founder, MIT/Tomorrow.io), Ryan Keisler (Chief Scientist, KoBold Metals). Advisor: Dr. Amy McGovern (Director, NSF AI2ES Institute).

**Data & Measurement Needs**:
- Primary data types: Satellite observations (NOAA/NASA NNJA archive), weather balloon radiosondes, surface station observations, ocean buoy data, reanalysis datasets
- Key measurements/parameters: Temperature (surface and atmospheric profiles), pressure, humidity, wind speed/direction, sea surface temperature, ocean currents and wave data
- Observation platforms/programs: NOAA/NASA Joint Archive (satellite + in-situ), Global Observing System (WMO/GOS), ECMWF reanalysis archives, AI-ready data format conversion from legacy archives
- Known data gaps: Legacy observational data in non-AI-ready formats, high-resolution ocean-atmosphere interface data, real-time data integration across heterogeneous sources
- Interest in external data services: High — actively building AI-ready datasets from NOAA/NASA archives; open to partnerships for additional observational data streams

**Connections**: NOAA partner. Part of growing AI weather prediction ecosystem including ECMWF, Google, and NVIDIA collaborations.
