# NeuralX

**Sector**: Aquaculture
**Official Site**: https://neuralx.ai/

## Overview
NeuralX provides "decision intelligence for aquaculture" — an AI platform that turns underwater camera feeds into actionable decisions on feeding, harvest timing, and fish health. Built by UCLA AI researcher Masaki Nakada, the company uses a proprietary 3D synthetic training data pipeline (Artificial Life Simulation Engine) to train models that no competitor can replicate. Ocean Exchange 2025 winner.

**Founded:** Based on DeepFoids research (NeurIPS 2022)
**Founder & CEO:** Masaki Nakada (PhD CS, UCLA; MIT TR35 Japan; Forbes Next 1000)
**Location:** Los Angeles, California
**Size:** 2-10 employees

## Business Model
**Performance-based pricing** — charges 25% of savings generated, with no fixed monthly fee:
- Minimum floor: $500/camera/month
- One-time installation: $1,500/camera
- Guaranteed ROI: 4.0× at every farm size
- 13-week payback period
- Works with existing underwater cameras (no hardware replacement)

### Savings Pillars
1. **Feed Savings**: Recovers 10%+ of feed cost (~60% of operating cost)
2. **Mortality Reduction**: Recovers 10%+ of disease-related biomass losses
3. **Inventory Accuracy**: Recovers 10%+ of revenue leakage from poor counting

## Key Technology
- **Artificial Life Simulation Engine**: Proprietary 3D synthetic training data pipeline — generates unlimited training data including rare events (disease outbreaks, extreme weather, equipment failures) that competitors can't capture in the wild
- **Hybrid Density + Detection Model**: Three complementary strategies (density estimation, bounding-box, point-based) combined adaptively
- **World Model + Decision Layer**: Simulates outcomes of policy changes (feeding, harvest timing, stocking density)
- Based on peer-reviewed science: DeepFoids (NeurIPS 2022), ACM SIGGRAPH, IEEE RA-L
- Dataset published at nere-us.com

## Data & Measurement Needs

### Primary data types
- Underwater video feeds (real-time, from existing cameras)
- Computer vision image streams (24/7 continuous monitoring)
- Fish behavioral data (swimming patterns, mouth movements, feed density)
- 3D synthetic training data (proprietary generation pipeline)

### Key measurements/parameters
- Fish count (real-time, individual tracking with unique IDs)
- Biomass estimation (92%+ accuracy, ±2cm length accuracy)
- Body length per fish (0.43cm error)
- Weight estimation (1.89g error)
- Satiation level / appetite index
- Health indicators: activity level, parasite presence, abnormal behavior
- Feed efficiency ratio
- Mortality rate and early disease detection (2-5 days pre-symptom)

### Observation platforms/programs
- Existing underwater IP cameras on farms (no proprietary hardware)
- Mobile and desktop dashboard applications (24/7 access)
- Integration with standard CCTV infrastructure
- Automated compliance logging (ASC/BAP certification ready)

### Known data gaps
- Requires existing camera infrastructure (does not provide hardware)
- 2-4 week calibration period needed per site
- Species-specific validation needed (currently validated for Kanpachi, Tiger Prawn, Salmon, Mackerel)
- Limited to farms doing $5M+ in annual revenue

### Interest in external data services
- Environmental water quality data (temperature, dissolved oxygen, turbidity) for correlating with fish behavior
- Oceanographic conditions for open-ocean farm sites
- Integration with farm management software (feeding systems, inventory systems)
- Satellite data for environmental context
