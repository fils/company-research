# Sofar Ocean

**Sector**: Ocean Data & AI
**Official Site**: https://www.sofarocean.com/

## Overview
Sofar Ocean operates the **largest privately-owned ocean sensor network in the world**, with 2,500+ Spotter drifters deployed globally capturing 1.5M real-time observations per day and 25M+ hours of ocean observations. The company provides ocean intelligence for maritime protection, efficiency, and forecasting — serving governments, defense, researchers, and commercial shipping.

## Business Model
- **Hardware + Data-as-a-Service**: Spotter drifter sales + ongoing data subscription
- **SaaS licensing**: Wayfinder voyage optimization (4-8% fuel reduction for shipping fleets)
- **Government/defense contracts**: NOAA, US Navy, ONR, MITRE
- **Marine weather forecasting**: proprietary models 50% more accurate than traditional
- **Autonomous survey services**: Spotter Scout mobile platforms
- **Commercial shipping**: data licensing to fleets (Eagle Bulk, MOL)

This is a **platform + data network** model — Sofar monetizes both the physical sensor network and the intelligence layer built on top of it. The network effect is self-reinforcing: more Spotters → more data → better models → more customers → more Spotters.

## Products

### Spotter Platform
Solar-powered ocean sensing buoys deployed globally. Surface sensing (waves, wind, weather) and subsurface sensing (sound, currents, temperature, water quality, water level). 2,500+ units deployed.

### Wayfinder
Voyage optimization software for commercial shipping. Combines live maritime weather data, vessel performance models, and commercial constraints. Delivers 4-8% average fuel reduction with daily routing and RPM guidance.

### Marine Weather
Proprietary ocean weather models built on Spotter network data. 50% more accurate than traditional forecasting models. Trusted by NOAA, US Navy, and the world's largest shipping fleets.

### Spotter Scout
Mobile autonomous surface platform for survey, transit, and station-keeping missions.

## Ocean Data & AI Sector Context
Sofar Ocean occupies a unique position as both an **ocean hardware company** (sensor manufacturing) and an **ocean data/AI company** (forecasting, voyage optimization). Unlike pure-software ocean AI plays (Amphitrite, Bluemvmt), Sofar owns the physical data collection layer — the Spotter network — giving it a proprietary data moat. This is analogous to Quartermaster's distributed vessel sensing network, but with purpose-built drifters rather than vessel-mounted hardware. The In-Q-Tel investment signals national security interest in private ocean data infrastructure.

### Data & Measurement Needs
- **Primary data types**: wave spectra, wind vectors, atmospheric pressure, sea surface temperature, subsurface acoustics, ocean currents, water quality parameters, water level
- **Key measurements/parameters**: significant wave height, wave period/direction, wind speed/direction, sea surface temperature, subsurface temperature profiles, ocean current speed/direction, dissolved oxygen, pH, turbidity, acoustic signatures
- **Observation platforms/programs**: Spotter drifter network (2,500+ units), Spotter Scout autonomous surface vehicles, satellite remote sensing integration, shipboard telemetry via Wayfinder
- **Known data gaps**: deep ocean subsurface coverage below Spotter drifter depth; Arctic ocean observation coverage; real-time data density in Southern Hemisphere; biogeochemical parameter coverage (limited to water quality basics)
- **Interest in external data services**: satellite altimetry, Argo float network integration, NOAA buoy network data, additional subsurface sensor partnerships, biogeochemical Argo (BGC-Argo) data for expanded ocean chemistry monitoring
