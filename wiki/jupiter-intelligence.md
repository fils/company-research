# Jupiter Intelligence

**Sector**: Climate Risk

**Official Site**: https://www.jupiterintel.com/

**Overview**  
Jupiter Intelligence provides decision-grade physical climate risk intelligence. The platform translates climate and extreme weather data into finance-aligned metrics (credit, loss, cashflow, ROI) to support capital allocation, underwriting, stress testing, and regulatory compliance.

**Core Product**  
**ClimateScore Global** – asset-level multi-peril projections (now to 2100) with 22k+ data points per location. Includes adaptation ROI modeling and transparent, MRM-approved analytics.

**Business Model**  
Enterprise SaaS platform sold to Global 100 companies, largest US banks, energy producers, and insurers. Strong focus on regulatory compliance (ECB, IFRS S2, EU Taxonomy).

**Marine / Coastal Relevance**  
Highly valuable for offshore energy operators, ports, and coastal infrastructure. Provides precise peril data used by companies such as Ørsted, BP, and Equinor.

**Clients**  
BP, Equinor, Eni, Liberty Mutual, Fannie Mae, Aon, Hawaiian Electric, and others.
