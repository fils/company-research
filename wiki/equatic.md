# Equatic

**Sector**: Ocean Carbon Sequestration

**Overview**  
Equatic has developed an electrolytic seawater process for permanent carbon removal. It stabilizes CO₂ as dissolved bicarbonate and solid mineral carbonates while co-producing green hydrogen as a revenue-generating byproduct.

**Technology**  
- Seawater electrolysis  
- Permanent storage in bicarbonate + mineral carbonates  
- Green hydrogen co-production  
- ISO 14064-2:2019 MRV methodology certified

**Business Model**  
Technology licensing + project development for large-scale ocean CDR. Revenue from carbon credits and hydrogen sales.

**Recent Highlights**  
- $11.6M Series A (2025)  
- Earthshot Prize finalist  
- First North American commercial-scale ocean CDR facility announced  
- Collaboration in Sarawak

**Marine Data Needs**  
Extremely high – relies on accurate carbonate chemistry, pH, and alkalinity monitoring at scale.
