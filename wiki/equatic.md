# Equatic

**Official Site**: https://equatic.tech/
**Last Updated**: 2026-05-26

**Focus**: Ocean-based Carbon Dioxide Removal via electrolysis of seawater, producing both carbon removal and green hydrogen.
**Key Tech**: Electrochemical seawater processing; separates dissolved CO₂ while generating hydrogen.
**Funding**: $11.6M Series A (2025); DOE grants; Times Top GreenTech 2026.
**Status**: World's largest ocean-based CDR plant planned in Singapore (UCLA Institute for Carbon Management partnership); targeting <$100/t CDR by commercial scale; pilot operations in Los Angeles and Singapore.

## Data & Measurement Needs

- **Primary data types**: Seawater chemistry (carbonate system), electrolysis process data, hydrogen production metrics, MRV verification data, environmental monitoring for discharge
- **Key measurements/parameters**: pH, DIC, alkalinity, dissolved inorganic carbon speciation, CO₂ concentration in exhaust, hydrogen purity, water temperature, salinity, flow rates, trace element composition of discharge
- **Observation platforms/programs**: Inline process sensors at electrolysis stacks, periodic water sampling at intake and discharge, MRV monitoring for carbon credit certification, environmental impact assessment sensors
- **Known data gaps**: Real-time carbonate chemistry sensors for process optimization; discharge plume dispersion monitoring; long-term ecosystem monitoring data for deployed sites
- **Interest in external data services**: High — MRV is critical for carbon credit sales; needs coastal seawater chemistry monitoring networks for site selection and baseline characterization
