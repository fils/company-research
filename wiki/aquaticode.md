# Aquaticode

**Sector**: Aquaculture
**Official Site**: https://aquaticode.com/

## Overview
Aquaticode is a Norway-based AI aquaculture company that builds machine learning and computer vision solutions to optimize farm production. Its core technology identifies performance traits — particularly gender — in juvenile salmon through non-invasive methods, enabling farmers to sort fish at high speed with >95% accuracy. The company is part of the NCE Seafood Innovation Cluster and backed by the Nacre Capital venture builder.

**Founded:** By Nacre Capital (venture builder)
**CEO:** Stian Rognlid
**Location:** Norway (with Chile operations)

## Business Model
Aquaticode sells hardware + software solutions to salmon farming operations:
- **SORTpro**: Automatic high-speed machine sorting 10,000 juvenile salmon/hour by gender and production traits
- **SORTvax**: Turns vaccination line-speed into a decision point — groups for robustness, removes unviable fish
- **Bass/bream solutions**: Launching 2026

Revenue from equipment sales to salmon farms. Transitioning from startup to scale-up with contracts to sort over 60 million fish.

## Data & Measurement Needs

### Primary data types
- Computer vision image data (millions of fish images for ML training)
- Video streams of fish on processing lines
- Phenotypic data (visual expression of DNA traits)
- Genotypic data (DNA analysis cross-reference)

### Key measurements/parameters
- Fish gender identification (>95% accuracy for 20-100g salmon)
- Fish weight and size grading
- Sexual maturation status
- Performance trait indicators (growth efficiency, robustness)
- Feed Conversion Ratio (FCR) optimization metrics
- Production volume and harvest yield

### Observation platforms/programs
- In-line camera systems on fish processing/sorting equipment
- Portable scanning devices (SORTmini)
- High-speed automated sorting machines (SORTpro)
- Land-based facility imaging for broodstock selection

### Known data gaps
- Currently limited to salmon; bass/bream expansion in 2026
- Relies on fish being in the 20-100g weight range for optimal accuracy
- Genotype-phenotype mapping still under research for non-salmon species
- Limited real-time environmental monitoring of farm conditions

### Interest in external data services
- Environmental monitoring data for farm optimization
- Oceanographic conditions affecting salmon aquaculture sites
- Integration with broader farm management systems
