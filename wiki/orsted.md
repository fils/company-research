# Ørsted

**Sector**: Offshore Energy
**Official Site**: https://orsted.com/

**Overview**
World's leading offshore wind developer, owner, and operator. Full value chain from development and construction to operations. Public company (OMX). Recent strategic shift to divest onshore assets to focus on offshore wind leadership.

**Technology**
- Offshore wind farm development and operation
- Full EPC capabilities
- Large-scale floating wind projects
- Marine environmental monitoring programs

**Business Model**
Power generation revenue + green energy certificates. Developer/owner/operator model with full lifecycle capabilities.

**Funding & Backers**
- Major public company listed on OMX Copenhagen
- Q1 2026 reporting; completed divestments of onshore assets

### Data & Measurement Needs
- Primary data types: Metocean data, marine environmental survey data, biodiversity monitoring
- Key measurements: Wind speed/direction, wave characteristics, current profiles, seafloor conditions, marine mammal presence, bird migration patterns
- Observation platforms: Dedicated metocean buoys, satellite remote sensing, ship-based surveys, AUV/ROV inspections
- Known data gaps: High-resolution pre-construction site characterization; real-time operational monitoring at scale
- Interest in external data services: Very high — every wind farm requires extensive metocean and environmental baseline surveys for permitting and operations
