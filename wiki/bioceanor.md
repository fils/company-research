# BiOceanOr

**Sector**: Aquaculture
**Official Site**: https://bioceanor.com/

## Overview

BiOceanOr is a French aquaculture AI company that combines biology and AI to deliver water quality forecasting and environmental intelligence for fish farmers. With 5 years of R&D validated at sea, 10M+ data points processed monthly, and deployments in 10+ countries, BiOceanOr helps aquaculture operators prevent hypoxia, optimize feeding and aeration, and protect animal welfare in the face of intensifying environmental pressures (oxygen depletion, harmful algal blooms, fluctuating feed intake, fish stress).

## Business Model

**SaaS subscription** for water quality monitoring and forecasting:
- **AquaCHECK** — data consistency QA (ensures sensor data reliability)
- **AquaFORECAST** — 12h-horizon oxygen forecast with 0.5 mg/L accuracy on average
- **AquaLYTICS** — cross-farm analytics for multi-site operations
- **PREDIMAR** — oxygen prediction system deployed for Chilean salmon industry

**Data integration**: pulls from real-time sensors, open/satellite/sampling data to produce forecasts and analytics.

**Multi-country deployment**: 10+ countries including Chilean salmon (PREDIMAR), Norwegian operations (Alexia Mouton country lead, Mar 2026).

**Domain expertise as moat**: 20+ experts (data scientists, oceanographers, marine biologists, fish health biologists) — the biology+AI combination differentiates from pure-software competitors.
