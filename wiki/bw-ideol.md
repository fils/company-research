# BW Ideol

**Sector**: Offshore Energy
**Official Site**: https://www.bw-ideol.com/

**Overview**
BW Ideol specializes in floating offshore wind platforms using the patented Damping Pool® technology. The design uses a central moonpool to reduce wave-induced motion, enabling lower-cost floating wind deployment in deeper waters.

**Technology**
- Damping Pool® floating platform — patented motion-reduction design
- Suitable for deeper waters
- Reduced steel weight vs. conventional floaters
- Standard quayside assembly and tow-out methodology

**Business Model**
Technology licensing + project development. B2B partnerships with offshore wind developers and utilities. Part of the BW Group, a global maritime conglomerate.

### Data & Measurement Needs
- Primary data types: Metocean characterization, site environmental surveys
- Key measurements: Wave spectra (height/period/direction), current profiles, wind resource, water depth
- Observation platforms: Metocean buoys, geotechnical surveys, environmental monitoring stations
- Known data gaps: Regional wave climate data needed for Damping Pool optimization
- Interest in external data services: High — platform motion characteristics depend on accurate wave data for each deployment site
