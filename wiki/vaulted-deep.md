# Vaulted Deep

**Sector**: Ocean Carbon Sequestration (BiCRS / Waste-to-Removal)

**Overview**  
Vaulted Deep provides a dual-purpose solution: managing challenging organic waste while removing carbon from the atmosphere for 10,000+ years. The company injects waste (manure, sewage sludge, paper sludge, agricultural byproducts) deep underground using proven oil & gas technology, preventing both local pollution and atmospheric emissions.

**Business Model**  
- Waste disposal fees + high-quality carbon credit sales  
- Major credit buyers: Stripe, Microsoft, Google, Meta, JPMorgan, Autodesk, H&M, Shopify  
- Scale achieved through existing industrial infrastructure

**Key Metrics (May 2026)**  
- 44,415+ tonnes CO₂ removed  
- Certified by Isometric  
- Two main facilities: Great Plains (Kansas) and TIRE (Los Angeles)

**Marine Relevance**  
Complementary to pure ocean CDR. Prevents land-based organic waste from entering coastal watersheds and oceans.
