# Orpheus Ocean

**Sector**: Marine Monitoring & Sensors
**Official Site**: https://orpheusocean.com/

## Overview
Orpheus Ocean provides flexible, cost-effective autonomous underwater vehicles (AUVs) for seafloor data collection and monitoring in Earth’s most extreme environments (up to 11,000m depth). Small-footprint AUV enables scalable data for marine resources, offshore infrastructure (wind), subsea defense. Eliminates vessel-dependent surveys. $2.8M Pre-Seed; InnovateMass award; partnerships with Seabed 2030, NOAA Ocean Exploration, WHOI heritage. New England-based (MA).

## Business Model
- AUV sales, lease/operations, data-as-a-service for deep-sea exploration and monitoring
- Grant-funded R&D + commercial deployments for offshore wind, critical minerals, defense
- Focus on affordability and accessibility for previously unreachable depths

## Funding
- $2.8M Pre-Seed (2025/2026)
- InnovateMass $276k + cost share for benthic monitoring/offshore wind
- Historical NOAA/OECI support

## Key Technology
- Orpheus AUV: agile multi-mode operations, seafloor-hopping, deep-rated
- Benthic monitoring, sub-bottom profiling, environmental sensing

### Data & Measurement Needs
- **Primary data types:** Seafloor imagery/bathymetry, environmental params, benthic ecosystem data, minerals
- **Key measurements/parameters:** Depth/temp/salinity/currents/sediment/biodiversity
- **Observation platforms/programs:** AUV fleets for Seabed 2030, NOAA, offshore wind permitting/MRV
- **Known data gaps:** Scalable high-res deep-sea baselines; real-time MRV telemetry
- **Interest in external data services:** Oceanographic feeds, sensor fusion, policy/MRV data integration
