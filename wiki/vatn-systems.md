# Vatn Systems

**Sector**: Marine Monitoring & Sensors
**Official Site**: https://www.vatnsystems.com/

**Overview**
Vatn Systems is a defense-technology company building affordable, modular autonomous underwater vehicles (AUVs) that operate in cooperative swarms for naval warfare, subsea surveillance, research, and commercial maritime missions. Founded 2023 in Rhode Island by maritime experts and military leaders. Mission: "become the next underwater defense prime" — building attritable (low-cost, expendable) AUVs in a world where autonomous systems dominate the naval battlespace.

**Technology**
- **Modular AUV platform** — flexible deployment by hand, air, or surface vessel
- **Advanced navigation software** — effective in GPS/vision/comms-denied environments
- **Autonomous, "humans optional"** — full autonomy, no human-in-the-loop
- **Cooperative swarming** — vehicles sync in time/space for complex missions
- **Stealthy low-bandwidth comms** between vehicles
- **Advanced path planning** for collaborative task completion
- **Sensor/data fusion** techniques that reduce error in inertial navigation
- **Modular building block structure** — tailored to range, accuracy, sensor payload
- **New AUV-torpedo product line** (unveiled alongside new manufacturing facility)

**Business Model**
- Sale/lease of attritable (low-cost, expendable) AUVs to defense customers
- Cooperative swarm mission services
- R&D contracts with US Navy, allied navies, research institutions
- Modular payload licensing for third-party sensor integration
- International defense sales (Singapore first international contract)

**Recent Highlights**
- **$60M Series A** (announced December 2025) — one of the largest AUV defense raises to date
- New state-of-the-art manufacturing facility in Rhode Island
- New AUV-torpedo product line unveiled
- First international contract win: Singapore
- Founding team includes maritime experts and military leaders

### Data & Measurement Needs
- Primary data types: AUV-mounted acoustic/optical/magnetic sensor data; cooperative swarm telemetry (stealthy low-bandwidth); bathymetric and seafloor mapping; subsea infrastructure surveillance (pipelines, cables, ports); multi-vehicle data fusion; inertial navigation error reduction data; subsea environmental monitoring; defense ISR data; edge AI for autonomy
- Key measurements/parameters: Acoustic signatures (active/passive sonar); magnetic anomalies; bathymetry/seafloor topography; current and tidal velocity profiles; subsea temperature and salinity; AUV position, heading, attitude; vessel/contact classification probabilities
- Observation platforms/programs: Cooperative AUV swarms (hand-launched, air-launched, surface-vessel-launched); modular sensor payloads; low-bandwidth inter-vehicle comms network; edge AI for onboard decision making
- Known data gaps: Scalable attritable AUV platforms for high-risk missions; cooperative swarm navigation in GPS/comm-denied undersea environments; low-cost inertial navigation error reduction for swarm deployments
- Interest in external data services: High — defense ISR, environmental monitoring, and post-mission analysis all benefit from external oceanographic and remote sensing data fusion
