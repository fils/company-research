## Amphitrite

**Overview**: Amphitrite (France) is an AI-powered ocean data intelligence company transforming complex oceanographic data into actionable intelligence. By fusing satellite data (SWOT altimetry, SAR, optical) with in-situ measurements and predictive AI, they deliver real-time ocean forecasts for commercial shipping, naval defence, and maritime operations.

**Funding**: €1.2M seed (2024). Supported by NVIDIA Inception, Microsoft GenAI Studio, Bpifrance, Space Founders, Polytechnique Incubator, Agoranov, and Atlantic Labs.

**Key Technology**:
- **Ocean Data Intelligence**: AI-powered data fusion combining satellite (SWOT, altimetry, SAR) + in-situ sensor data
- **Ocean Bulletin**: Route planning and maritime operations tool
- **Green Shipping**: Fuel efficiency optimization via AI-driven routing
- **Naval Defence**: Maritime situational awareness for sovereignty

**Key Partners**: SHOM (French Naval Hydrographic Service), Mercator Ocean International, CNES, ESA, CLS, Subsea7, CMA CGM, Louis Dreyfus Armateurs, Heerema, Orange Marine, DGA

**Data & Measurement Needs**:
- Primary data types: Satellite data (SWOT, altimetry, SAR, optical imagery), in-situ oceanographic measurements, maritime traffic data (AIS), bathymetric data, metocean/weather forecasts
- Key measurements/parameters: Sea surface height and ocean topography (SWOT), SST, ocean currents (velocity, direction), wind speed/direction, wave height/period/direction, salinity, water density profiles
- Observation platforms/programs: Real-time satellite data ingestion (SWOT, altimetry constellation, SAR), in-situ buoy network integration, AI-powered data fusion combining multi-source streams, Ocean Bulletin route optimization
- Known data gaps: Sub-surface oceanographic data at depth, high-resolution coastal bathymetry, real-time in-situ data from data-sparse regions
- Interest in external data services: Very high — core product depends on fusing diverse ocean data sources

**Cross-sector relevance**: Major ocean data intelligence platform serving multiple blue economy sectors. Partners include leading maritime, space, and defence organizations. Complements **Apeiron Labs** (AUV-based ocean data) and **Oshen** (autonomous ocean robots) in the ocean data platform ecosystem.
