# Hullbot

**Sector**: Marine Monitoring & Sensors
**Official Site**: https://www.hullbot.com/

## Overview
Hullbot builds autonomous underwater robots that provide frequent hull cleaning and inspection services for commercial and recreational fleets. The company's proactive "grooming" approach prevents biofouling buildup, reducing drag and fuel consumption while increasing fleet performance. Founded in Sydney, Australia, Hullbot operates a cloud robotics platform with real-time analytics and delivers services globally.

## Business Model
- **Autonomous frequent cleaning as-a-service**: No upfront cost to vessel operators; ongoing service relationship
- **Proactive grooming paradigm**: Shift from reactive cleaning (diminishing returns) to proactive grooming (prevents fouling before it occurs)
- **Cloud robotics**: Real-time analytics and fleet performance data recording over time
- **Global service**: Delivering services globally with international hubs

## Key Technology
- **Autonomous underwater robot**: Advanced underwater robotics platform with powerful magnetic grip for hull adhesion
- **Gentle brush system**: Safe for all coatings (including silicone); cleaning speed, pressures, and brush type adjusted per vessel
- **Cloud robotics platform**: Real-time analytics, asset intelligence, fleet performance tracking
- **Manoeuvrability**: Unparalleled manoeuvrability enabling cleaning of propellers and niche areas
- **Day and night operations**: Flexible scheduling with simple deployment

## Data & Measurement Needs
- **Primary data types**: Hull condition imaging, biofouling assessment data, vessel drag/fuel consumption telemetry, underwater inspection imagery
- **Key measurements/parameters**: Hull roughness, fouling type and coverage percentage, drag coefficient changes, fuel consumption before/after cleaning, coating wear metrics, propeller condition
- **Observation platforms/programs**: Autonomous robot-mounted cameras and sensors for hull condition monitoring; cloud-based longitudinal fleet performance tracking; routine inspection data without divers
- **Known data gaps**: Standardized biofouling impact metrics across vessel types and coatings; correlation between cleaning frequency and long-term coating durability; environmental impact of cleaning in different water bodies
- **Interest in external data services**: Water quality data for cleaning compliance (regulatory liaison); port and harbor environmental data; vessel performance benchmarking data

## Cross-Sector Connections
- **Marine Monitoring & Sensors**: Hullbot's underwater robots are sensor platforms — overlap with Boxfish Robotics (hovering AUV), MarineSitu (underwater cameras), Bedrock Ocean Exploration (seafloor mapping AUV)
- **Maritime Operations**: Direct customer base includes ferry operators (FRS Clipper, Cruise Whitsundays), commercial fleets — connects to Orca AI and Sea Machines (vessel autonomy/navigation)
- **Offshore Energy**: Hull cleaning reduces fuel consumption — supports maritime decarbonization alongside Fleetzero (electric propulsion) and Kvasir Technologies (biofuel)
