# Saronic Technologies

**Sector**: Marine Monitoring & Sensors
**Official Site**: https://www.saronic.com/

## Overview
Saronic Technologies is a defense-tech company designing and manufacturing autonomous surface vessels (ASVs) for maritime autonomy. Founded in 2023 and based in Austin, Texas, Saronic has rapidly scaled to become one of the most well-funded defense startups in the US, reaching a $9.25B valuation with its March 2026 $1.75B Series D.

## Business Model
- **Direct government/military sales**: US Navy production contracts (MUSV Marketplace, OTA agreements), allied navy sales
- **Vessel manufacturing at scale**: modular ASV production with mission-specific payload configuration
- **Software licensing**: Mission Control autonomy suite, AI-driven navigation and multi-asset teaming
- **International partnerships**: Taiwan (NCSIST), UK/EU/Australia (Lloyd's Register)
- **Defense procurement**: OTA, production agreements through mid-2031

This is a **defense prime manufacturing** model — Saronic builds and sells physical autonomous vessels plus software to government/military customers, with expanding international sales channels.

## Defense Marine Autonomy Context
Saronic represents the **scaling phase** of the defense marine autonomy sector. Alongside HavocAI ($100M Series A, ~$200M total), Vatn Systems ($60M Series A), and Ulysses ($46M from a16z), Saronic's $2.6B total funding dwarfs all peers. The company is moving from startup to prime contractor status — building dedicated shipyards, securing multi-year Navy production contracts, and expanding internationally. This signals sector maturation from R&D to fleet-level deployment.

### Data & Measurement Needs
- **Primary data types**: real-time sea state data, wave spectra, wind vectors, current profiles, bathymetric data, meteorological/oceanographic (METOC) feeds
- **Key measurements/parameters**: significant wave height, wave period/direction, wind speed/direction, ocean current speed/direction, water depth, sea surface temperature, sea state classification
- **Observation platforms/programs**: onboard ASV sensor suites, satellite communications, multichannel comms in contested environments, digital twin simulation environments
- **Known data gaps**: persistent wide-area oceanographic coverage for autonomous ASV operations; real-time environmental data for autonomous navigation in extreme sea states; subsurface oceanographic data for multi-domain operations
- **Interest in external data services**: METOC data feeds for mission planning and autonomous navigation; satellite oceanographic data for route optimization; acoustic environment data for underwater domain awareness
