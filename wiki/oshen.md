# Oshen

**Sector**: Offshore Energy / Ocean Data
**Official Site**: https://www.oshendata.com/

**Overview**
Oshen develops C-Stars — 1-meter autonomous sailing robots deployable in swarms for persistent, wide-area ocean intelligence. Proven in Category 5 hurricanes with NOAA. Addresses the critical ocean data gap by offering scalable constellations at dramatically lower cost than traditional buoys and ships.

**Technology**
- C-Star: 1m autonomous sailing robot, multi-month endurance
- Swarm/constellation architecture for spatial density
- Edge processing with satellite data transmission
- Custom-configurable sensor payloads
- Extreme environment engineering

**Business Model**
Hardware (C-Star) + data subscription. Constellation-as-a-service for government agencies, offshore energy, research institutions, and maritime security.

**Recent Highlights**
- First ocean robot to collect data in a Category 5 hurricane
- NOAA contracts for hurricane monitoring
- £2M UK ARIA project for Forecasting Tipping Points
- Partnership with University of Southern Mississippi

### Data & Measurement Needs
- Primary data types: Metocean (waves, wind, currents), atmospheric pressure, acoustic data, SST
- Key measurements: Wave height/period/direction, wind speed/direction, barometric pressure, SST, current profiles, acoustic signatures
- Observation platforms: C-Star constellations, edge computing + satellite uplink
- Known data gaps: Massive spatial gaps in current ocean monitoring; delayed data delivery; high cost of traditional observation
- Interest in external data services: Core business — Oshen IS the data collection solution; can partner with CDR companies for real-time metocean/MRV data
