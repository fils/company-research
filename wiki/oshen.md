# Oshen

**Sector**: Offshore Energy / Ocean Data
**Official Site**: https://www.oshendata.com/
**Last Updated**: 2026-09-09

**Overview**
Oshen develops C-Stars — 1-meter autonomous sailing robots deployable in swarms for persistent, wide-area ocean intelligence. Proven in Category 5 hurricanes with NOAA. Addresses the critical ocean data gap by offering scalable constellations at dramatically lower cost than traditional buoys and ships. Plymouth, UK; team ~30. CEO/co-founder Anahita Laverack.

**Technology**
- C-Star: 1m autonomous sailing robot, multi-month endurance; wind + solar powered
- Swarm/constellation architecture for spatial density
- Edge processing with satellite data transmission
- Custom-configurable sensor payloads
- Extreme environment engineering (hurricane-class)

**Business Model**
Hardware (C-Star) + data subscription. Constellation-as-a-service for government agencies, offshore energy, research institutions, and maritime security. Customers disclosed include the **US Navy, Royal Navy, and UK Met Office**.

**Funding & Scale**
- **£3.65M / ~$5M equity (Aug 2026)** led by **Lunar Ventures** with AlbionVC, Twin Track, Concept Ventures + angels — use of proceeds: **triple manufacturing rate** (CEO: production as "fast-food kitchen not shipyard")
- Prior: £2M UK ARIA project for Forecasting Tipping Points; NOAA hurricane contracts
- Partnership with University of Southern Mississippi

**Recent Highlights**
- First ocean robot to collect data in a Category 5 hurricane
- Manufacturing bottleneck thesis: sensing proven; scale is the product
- Positions as the persistent sensing layer partner for offshore energy MRV ([Bluecore Energy](bluecore-energy.md), wind operators, CDR)

### Data & Measurement Needs
- Primary data types: Metocean (waves, wind, currents), atmospheric pressure, acoustic data, SST
- Key measurements: Wave height/period/direction, wind speed/direction, barometric pressure, SST, current profiles, acoustic signatures
- Observation platforms: C-Star constellations, edge computing + satellite uplink
- Known data gaps: Massive spatial gaps in current ocean monitoring; delayed data delivery; high cost of traditional observation; factory throughput for swarm density
- Interest in external data services: Core business — Oshen IS the data collection solution; natural partner for CDR/offshore energy MRV and weather agencies
