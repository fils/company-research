# Tidal

**Sector**: Aquaculture
**Official Site**: https://www.tidalx.ai/en

## Overview
Tidal (TidalX AI Inc.) is an AI-powered underwater vision and robotics company for aquaculture, spun out from Alphabet's X (the moonshot factory) in August 2024. Tidal's Orca camera system provides real-time biomass estimation, sea lice detection, fish welfare monitoring, and autonomous feeding for salmon farms. With 300+ cameras deployed with Mowi (world's largest Atlantic salmon producer), Tidal is a leading AI aquaculture company with Google Cloud infrastructure and NS9415 hardware certification.

## Business Model
- **Hardware + SaaS**: Orca camera system deployment with enterprise AI platform subscription
- **Per-pen deployment**: camera systems installed in aquaculture pens
- **Data-driven insights licensing**: biomass, health, welfare analytics
- **Strategic enterprise partnerships**: deep integration with major salmon producers (Mowi, SalMar)
- **Autonomous feeding optimization**: feed cost reduction through AI-driven feeding decisions

This is a **hardware-enabled SaaS** model — Tidal deploys physical camera systems in pens and charges for the AI analytics platform. The Google Cloud infrastructure and enterprise-grade security differentiate from smaller competitors. Strategic partnerships with Mowi (300+ cameras) and SalMar create deep moats through proprietary training data.

## Aquaculture AI Sector Context
Tidal occupies the **enterprise-grade, Google-backed** tier of aquaculture AI. Compared to competitors:
- **Aquabyte**: also computer vision + biomass monitoring, but Tidal has Alphabet heritage and Google Cloud infrastructure
- **NeuralX**: performance-based pricing (25% of savings) vs. Tidal's enterprise SaaS; NeuralX has synthetic training data moat
- **Aquaticode**: gender sorting and vaccination vs. Tidal's welfare/feeding focus
- **ReelData AI**: land-based farm focus vs. Tidal's sea-pen focus
- **Kurma AI**: GenAI foundation model approach vs. Tidal's specialized vision system

Tidal's unique advantage is **individual fish identification** ("fishal recognition") — enabling precision aquaculture at the individual animal level, which is a step beyond population-level monitoring.

### Data & Measurement Needs
- **Primary data types**: underwater video/image streams, fish biomass data, sea lice count imagery, fish behavior telemetry, feeding data, environmental sensor data (pen-level)
- **Key measurements/parameters**: fish biomass (weight), fish length, sea lice count per fish, fish welfare indicators, feed conversion ratio, water temperature (pen), dissolved oxygen (pen), salinity (pen), fish swimming behavior patterns
- **Observation platforms/programs**: Orca autonomous robotic cameras (300+ deployed with Mowi), pen-mounted sensor arrays, Google Cloud data processing pipeline
- **Known data gaps**: open-ocean baseline environmental data for comparison with pen conditions; real-time water quality at pen depth (beyond camera-mounted sensors); ecosystem-level impact monitoring beyond farm perimeter; oceanographic context for site-level decision-making
- **Interest in external data services**: oceanographic data for site selection, environmental baseline data for regulatory compliance, satellite SST/chlorophyll for farm environment context, metocean data for pen structural integrity
