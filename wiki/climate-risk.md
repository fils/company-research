# Climate Risk

**Overview (as of May 2026)**

Climate risk intelligence providers in the knowledge graph specialize in translating physical climate hazards into financial and operational impacts. They serve banks, insurers, real estate investors, asset managers, and energy companies with decision-grade data. **Brightband** joins the sector as a new entrant focused on the upstream — AI-driven weather and climate forecasting infrastructure.

**Core Themes**
- Property- and asset-level risk modeling
- Physical peril projections (flood, wildfire, extreme heat, wind, sea-level rise)
- Financial translation (loss, credit, cashflow, ROI)
- Regulatory compliance support (ECB, IFRS S2, EU Taxonomy)
- Adaptation planning and resilience investment modeling
- **NEW**: AI weather/climate forecasting infrastructure and open-source datasets

**Companies Profiled**

- **Jupiter Intelligence**: Decision-grade physical risk platform (ClimateScore Global). Serves Global 100 companies, major US banks and energy firms. Strong focus on transparent, MRM-approved analytics for capital allocation and stress testing.

- **First Street Foundation**: Property-level climate risk modeling. Provides transparent physics-based deterministic scores widely adopted by Fannie Mae, Zillow, major banks, and government agencies. Strong wildfire and flood coverage.

- **Kettle**: Specialized AI-driven wildfire insurance and reinsurance (California focus). Uses massive satellite + weather datasets to predict ignition, spread, and vulnerability with >97% historical accuracy.

- **Climate X**: Physical climate risk analytics platform (Spectra, Adapt, Carta). Serves institutions managing trillions in AUM. Focus on adaptation ROI modeling and corporate asset mapping (18.7M companies).

- **Brightband**: AI weather & climate forecasting startup. Building an end-to-end AI Earth System for probabilistic forecasting. $10M Series A (Prelude Ventures, Starshot Capital, CLAI Ventures). NOAA partner for NNJA-AI observational data archive. Team from Google X, MIT, KoBold Metals. Open-source benchmark weather datasets.

**Marine / Coastal Relevance**
- Many clients include ports, coastal real estate, offshore energy infrastructure, and marine terminal operators.
- Sea-level rise and coastal flooding are core perils.
- Strong data synergy with offshore energy companies (Ørsted, Principle Power, BW Ideol, Gazelle).
- Brightband's AI weather forecasting tools directly benefit offshore wind and maritime operations.
