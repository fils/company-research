# Coastal Measures

**Sector**: Coastal Risk & Intelligence
**Official Site**: https://www.coastalmeasures.com/

## Overview
Coastal Measures provides the CUMULUS platform delivering coastal risk analytics, hydrokinetic site identification, and resilient community intelligence using AI. Anchored in Maine's blue economy with Kittery HQ.

## Business Model
AI SaaS platform matching projects to intelligence for investors and marine businesses; data-driven decision support for coastal resilience and sustainable ocean economy.

## Funding
$260K seed round from Maine Angels (2026)

## Key Technology
AI analytics engine for integrating coastal environmental data, predictive risk modeling, geospatial intelligence.

### Data & Measurement Needs
- **Primary data types:** Coastal sensor streams, satellite imagery, hydrodynamic model outputs, community infrastructure data
- **Key measurements/parameters:** Wave height, current speed, sea level rise projections, coastal erosion rates, hydrokinetic resource potential (tidal/wave energy)
- **Observation platforms/programs:** Coastal monitoring networks, real-time sensor buoys, satellite remote sensing validation
- **Known data gaps:** High-resolution localized coastal risk data at community scale; integrated multi-hazard models
- **Interest in external data services:** NOAA coastal data portals, Copernicus marine services, public hydrographic surveys

## Connections
Bridges coastal communities with blue economy investors; complements ocean data platforms.
