# Coastal Measures

**Sector**: Ocean Data & AI
**Official Site**: https://www.coastalmeasures.com/
**HQ**: Greenland, NH, USA
**Last Updated**: 2026-05-17

## Overview

Coastal Measures provides **CUMULUS**, a coastal intelligence platform powered by **YSOK AI**, that unifies multi-modal coastal sensor data into a governed data fabric. The platform turns raw sensor data from radars, cameras, buoys, and satellites into actionable intelligence for risk analysis, clean energy siting, resilience planning, and aquaculture management.

**Tagline:** "Better ocean intelligence saves time, money, and lives."

## Business Model

SaaS platform for coastal data aggregation and intelligence. Revenue from platform subscriptions, API access, and partner integrations with sensor vendors, government agencies, insurers, and coastal operators.

## Key Technology

- **CUMULUS Data Fabric** — Streaming + historical ingestion with automated metadata capture, normalization, QC, and retention
- **YSOK AI** — Ocean-specific AI engine for narratives, anomaly detection, and scenario planning; tuned for ocean physics, remote sensing, and coastal missions
- **Explainable AI** — Outputs designed for insurance underwriting, permitting, and policy applications
- **Digital Twin Capabilities** — Parcel-level surge, erosion, and wave analytics
- **<1 Day Sensor Integration** — Multi-vendor sensor onboarding replacing weeks of custom engineering

## Data & Measurement Needs

- **Primary data types**: Coastal radar feeds, camera/satellite imagery, buoy telemetry (wave height, wind, currents), tidal flood sensors, water quality sensors, hydrokinetic velocity fields, bathymetry data
- **Key measurements/parameters**: Storm surge elevation, erosion rates, wave energy/hydrokinetic yield, water quality (HAB indicators, temp, DO, salinity), wind vectors, precipitation, temperature stratification
- **Observation platforms/programs**: NOAA IOOS observing network, USACE coastal monitoring stations, Sofar Ocean Spotter buoys, third-party sensor deployments, satellite remote sensing (ESA, NOAA)
- **Known data gaps**: Automated QA/QC for multi-vendor sensor networks (solved by CUMULUS); real-time coastal hazard detection at parcel level; standardized multi-modal data governance
- **Interest in external data services**: Core business — CUMULUS platform designed to ingest any coastal sensor feed and make it AI-ready
