# Pronoe

**Sector**: Ocean Carbon Sequestration
**Official Site**: https://www.pronoe.earth/

## Overview
Pronoe is a French ocean carbon removal company developing automated water treatment systems that restore the ocean's natural capacity to permanently remove CO2 from the air. Their asset-light approach integrates electrochemical alkalinity enhancement technology downstream from existing industrial sites, processing return flows to the sea into less acidic or more alkaline flows. This co-locates CDR with industrial infrastructure, avoiding the need for dedicated marine vessels or offshore platforms.

**Location:** France
**Affiliations:** Bpifrance, French Tech, DeepTech, EIT, AirMiners, Frontier Climate portfolio, MIT, INSEAD

## Business Model
- Sells **high-quality CO2 removal certificates** (carbon credits)
- Deploys **automated water treatment systems** at industrial sites with coastal discharge
- **Asset-light**: leverages existing industrial infrastructure (no dedicated marine operations)
- Industrial site partners benefit from regulatory compliance and carbon removal credits
- Mitigates coastal acidification as a direct co-benefit

## Funding

  - Initial $500K catalytic pre-purchase (2025)
  - Expanded to $3.05M with Google participation
  - Frontier's **first French portfolio company**
- Affiliated with: Bpifrance, French Tech, EIT, MIT, INSEAD

## Key Technology
- **Electrochemical alkalinity enhancement** of industrial discharge streams
- Uses electricity to process return flows → less acidic / more alkaline
- Automated, seamlessly integrated downstream from industrial sites
- Compliant with current applicable regulations
- Issues verified CO2 removal certificates
- Leverages third-party infrastructure

## Key Differentiators
- **Asset-light**: No dedicated marine vessels or offshore platforms
- **Co-located with industry**: Uses existing industrial outflows
- **Coastal acidification mitigation**: Direct environmental co-benefit beyond CDR
- **Scalable**: Deployable at any industrial site with coastal discharge
- **French deep-tech ecosystem**: Supported by Bpifrance, French Tech, EIT
