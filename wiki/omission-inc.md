## Business Model
Omission Inc manufactures portable autonomous surface vessels (ASVs) and unmanned aerial systems (UAS). Their ASVs reduce nearshore marine data collection costs by 50–70%, providing survey-grade marine data for environmental monitoring, coastal surveys, and aquaculture applications. They also develop unmanned ground vehicles and operate across defense, research, and commercial marine sectors.

## Data & Measurement Needs
- Primary data types: Hydrographic survey data, bathymetric measurements, water quality sensor streams, nearshore environmental data, acoustic data
- Key measurements/parameters: Water depth, temperature, salinity, dissolved oxygen, turbidity, wave height, current velocity, substrate composition
- Observation platforms/programs: ASV deployments, UAS aerial surveys, autonomous sensor networks, fixed and mobile monitoring stations
- Known data gaps: Cost-prohibitive vessel-based surveys for small operators; limited temporal resolution for rapidly-changing nearshore conditions; data gaps in shallow/rocky coastal zones where traditional survey vessels can't operate
- Interest in external data services: NOAA coastal mapping data, satellite sea surface temperature, regional ocean model outputs, tidal current predictions, AIS vessel traffic data
