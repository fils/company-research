# Calcarea

**Sector**: Ocean Carbon Sequestration
**Official Site**: https://calcarea.com/

## Overview
Caltech/USC spun-out developing ship-board carbon capture via Accelerated Weathering of Limestone (AWL). Converts captured CO2 into oceanic bicarbonate — stable, secure, and environmentally benign. Eliminates port infrastructure dependency for pure CO2 handling.

## Business Model
- Marine on-board CCS technology for shipping
- Ocean CDR via bicarbonate discharge
- Revenue: technology licensing + carbon credits

## Funding
- **Seed**: $3.5M
- **Investors**: Propeller VC, Grantham Foundation, Azolla Ventures, LomarLabs, Kibo Invest, ZeBox (CMA CGM), Caltech, USC, AltaSea

## Key Technology
- AWL reactor for marine carbon capture
- Bicarbonate conversion and ocean discharge
- Smaller and more efficient than existing marine CCS

## Data & Measurement Needs
- Primary data types: In-situ carbonate chemistry, oceanographic sensor data, marine environmental baselines
- Key measurements: pH, DIC, total alkalinity, bicarbonate concentration, temperature, salinity, dissolved oxygen
- Observation platforms: Ship-board sensors, ocean discharge monitoring, water quality samplers
- Known data gaps: Real-time bicarbonate tracking in discharge zones, ecological impact baselines for AWL
- Interest in external data services: High — needs continuous ocean chemistry monitoring and environmental impact verification
