# FishLAT (Blue Latitudes)

**Sector**: Marine Monitoring & Sensors
**Official Site**: https://www.fishlat.com/

## Overview
FishLAT is a machine-learning-powered rapid assessment tool that predicts how the removal, reefing, or installation of offshore infrastructure will impact the environment and fisheries. Developed by Blue Latitudes LLC to bring data transparency to offshore development.

## Business Model
- **Product:** ML-based environmental impact assessment software-as-a-service
- **Markets:** Offshore energy (oil & gas, wind), regulatory agencies, environmental consultants, decommissioning contractors
- **Stage:** Commercial — has supported permitting of reefing sites
- **Funding:** VentureWell/NOAA OEA Stage 2 (Spring 2026) — $50K TDC award

## Key Technology
- Machine learning model trained on fisheries and environmental datasets
- Predictive assessment for three scenarios: structure removal, reefing, and new installation
- Rapid, data-rich, cost-effective alternative to traditional environmental surveys

### Data & Measurement Needs
- **Primary data types:** Fisheries catch data, marine species distribution, benthic habitat data, offshore infrastructure specs
- **Key measurements/parameters:** Fisheries stock assessments, marine ecosystem baseline data, species biodiversity metrics, benthic community composition
- **Observation platforms:** External fisheries surveys, satellite data, public databases (NOAA fisheries data, state fish & wildlife data)
- **Known data gaps:** Real-time fisheries data streams; high-resolution benthic mapping for offshore decommissioning sites; standardized environmental impact datasets across jurisdictions
- **Interest in external data services:** NOAA fisheries data, satellite-derived oceanographic data, public environmental impact databases, AIS vessel traffic data for fishing activity analysis

FishLAT has successfully supported the permitting of reefing sites, demonstrating real-world regulatory use for decommissioning and artificial reef decisions.
