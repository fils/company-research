# Captura

**Official Site**: https://capturacorp.com/
**Last Updated**: 2026-05-17

**Focus**: Direct Ocean Capture (DOC) using bipolar membrane electrodialysis.
**Status**: 1 kt/yr pilot operational in Hawaii; commercial scaling validated by Equinor.
**Backers & News**: TIME Top GreenTech 2026; XPRIZE; Frontier/Stripe/Equinor; new Cura partnership for cement decarbonization.

## Data & Measurement Needs

- **Primary data types**: Real-time seawater chemistry (pH, DIC, alkalinity), discharge water quality, MRV verification data, ecosystem safety monitoring data
- **Key measurements/parameters**: pH, DIC, total alkalinity, dissolved oxygen, temperature, salinity, current velocity at discharge points, CO₂ flux
- **Observation platforms/programs**: Hawaii pilot sensor network (continuous seawater chemistry), Equinor joint monitoring program, MRV stacks for carbon credit accounting
- **Known data gaps**: Long-term ecosystem impact data for DOC operations; continuous pH monitoring across depth profiles at commercial scale; standardized MRV protocols for ocean CDR
- **Interest in external data services**: High — comprehensive MRV is essential for carbon credit verification; needs coastal observation network integration
