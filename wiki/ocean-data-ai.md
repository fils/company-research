# Ocean Data & AI

**Overview (as of May 2026)**

Ocean Data & AI is a new sector in the knowledge graph covering companies whose primary business is providing AI-powered ocean data intelligence platforms and services. These companies sit at the intersection of oceanographic data collection, satellite remote sensing, AI/ML analytics, and blue economy decision support — distinct from companies that *use* ocean data for their core operations (CDR, offshore energy, aquaculture).

**Core Themes**
- AI-powered data fusion across satellite, in-situ, and AIS data sources
- Real-time ocean intelligence for commercial decision-making
- Maritime operations optimization (shipping efficiency, naval situational awareness)
- Satellite-derived oceanographic products (SWOT, altimetry, SAR, optical)
- Bridging the gap between public ocean data and commercial applications

**Companies Profiled**

- **Amphitrite**: AI-powered ocean data intelligence platform. Combines satellite data (SWOT, altimetry, SAR) with in-situ measurements and predictive AI for real-time maritime intelligence. Serves commercial shipping, naval defence, and offshore operations. €1.2M seed (2024). Partners include SHOM, CNES, ESA, Mercator Ocean, CMA CGM, Subsea7. Supported by NVIDIA Inception and Microsoft GenAI Studio.

**Cross-Sector Synergies**

This sector is highly complementary to every other sector in the knowledge graph:
- **Ocean Carbon Sequestration**: MRV data needs — Amphitrite's ocean intelligence delivers baseline oceanographic parameters (SST, currents, pH proxy data)
- **Offshore Energy**: Metocean data for wind farm siting and operations
- **Aquaculture**: Environmental monitoring for lease compliance and operations
- **Climate Risk**: Ocean-atmosphere data integration for climate modeling

**Related Companies** (borderline Ocean Data & AI)
- **Apeiron Labs** (currently in Ocean Carbon Sequestration): AUV-based ocean data platform — could also fit here
- **Oshen** (currently in Offshore Energy): C-Star autonomous ocean robots for persistent ocean intelligence — has a strong data services component
- **Brightband** (currently in Climate Risk): AI weather data platform with ocean data components

**Market Context**
- Global ocean intelligence market: estimated $2.3B (2025), growing to $18.7B by 2040
- Growing demand from maritime shipping decarbonization (EU ETS, FuelEU Maritime)
- Naval/double-use applications driving investment
- Increasing availability of open satellite data (Copernicus, SWOT, Sentinel) creating opportunities for value-add AI layers
