# SeaO2

**Sector**: Ocean Carbon Sequestration  
**Official Site**: https://www.seao2.com/  
**Last Updated**: 2026-05-03  

**Overview**  
SeaO2 is developing electrochemical Direct Ocean Capture (DOC) technology to extract CO₂ directly from seawater at gigaton scale using renewable electricity and seawater only. The process returns decarbonized water to the ocean surface, which re-absorbs atmospheric CO₂, simultaneously addressing ocean acidification. Post-2025 North Sea pilot.

**Business Model**  
- Sale of permanent, high-quality CDR credits (Isometric DOCS protocol).  
- Green CO₂ sales for utilization (methanol, chemicals, concrete mineralization, food/beverage).  
- Targeting corporate buyers and storage partners (geological formations).  
- 2026 pilots planned at 25 tCO₂/year nameplate capacity per system.

**Key Technology**  
- Electrochemical CO₂ extraction from seawater (no added chemicals).  
- Renewable-powered vacuum/membrane process.  
- CDR pathway (geological storage) + CO₂ utilization pathways.  
- Focus on local acidification reversal.

### Data & Measurement Needs
- **Primary data types**: Seawater carbonate chemistry, electrolyte and flow data, renewable energy integration telemetry.
- **Key measurements/parameters**: pH, DIC, total alkalinity, temperature, salinity, dissolved oxygen, CO₂ partial pressure, current speed; post-treatment water chemistry.
- **Observation platforms/programs**: Real-time sensor networks on pilot platforms, North Sea monitoring buoys, geological storage site validation, marine ecosystem surveys.
- **Known data gaps**: Affordable high-accuracy real-time carbonate monitoring at scale, long-term impact on local carbonate chemistry and biota.

**Marine Data Pain Points**  
Ocean-based electrochemical processes require continuous, precise marine carbonate chemistry data for process control, MRV, environmental impact assessment, and to demonstrate acidification mitigation benefits.

**Contacts / Notes**  
Dutch company. Recognized by European Commission (BlueInvest). Active in Platform Zero (South Holland). Developing 2026 standalone pilots. Strong science-led productization.
