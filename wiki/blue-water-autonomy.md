# Blue Water Autonomy

**Sector**: Marine Monitoring & Sensors
**Official Site**: https://www.blw.ai/

## Overview

Blue Water Autonomy builds autonomous warships for the U.S. Navy — full-size unmanned surface vessels designed for mass production and months-long endurance on the open ocean. Founded in 2024 in Boston by repeat entrepreneurs and Navy veterans, the company is modernizing U.S. shipbuilding through a platform strategy focused on perfecting a single vessel class (Liberty) with full-stack integration of hardware, software, and AI from the keel up.

## Business Model

**Defense prime contractor**: builds autonomous warships for U.S. Navy as the primary customer; Pentagon has $2.1B in new Congressional funding for medium-sized unmanned surface vessels (MUSVs). Mass-production-first design philosophy — "intentional strategy" of perfecting a single platform class for unmatched quality, speed to market, and reliability.

**Dual-use trajectory**: starting with naval applications, leadership sees massive commercial maritime opportunity in "dull, dangerous, and dirty jobs at sea." Platform designed for varied payloads complementing traditional naval assets.

## Data & Measurement Needs

### Primary data types
- Oceanographic sensor data for autonomous navigation (sea state, currents, wind)
- Met-ocean data for route planning and safety
- Bathymetric / seafloor data for navigation
- GPS-denied navigation reference data (for contested environments)

### Key measurements/parameters
- Wave height and period
- Current speed and direction
- Wind speed/direction
- Sea state
- Persistent position/navigation in GPS-denied scenarios

### Observation platforms/programs
- Autonomous vessel onboard sensors (meteorological, oceanographic, navigation)
- Persistent open-ocean monitoring for months-long missions
- Modular payload sensor suites (defense + environmental)

### Known data gaps
- Persistent wide-area ocean observation for autonomous navigation in contested environments
- Real-time oceanographic data for autonomous vessels operating independently for months at a time

### Interest in external data services
- Met-ocean forecasts for autonomous route planning and safety
- Satellite ocean data for persistent monitoring and situational awareness

## Context

Blue Water Autonomy enters the Marine Monitoring & Sensors sector alongside other defense ASV primes (Saronic Technologies $2.6B, HavocAI ~$200M, Seasats >$40M). What distinguishes Blue Water is its **full-size warship** focus (190-ft Liberty-class) vs smaller ASVs, and its **mass-production-first** philosophy led by repeat hardware entrepreneurs (6 River Systems/Shopify exit, iRobot/Roomba scaling). The Pentagon's $2.1B MUSV funding and China shipbuilding gap (200x US capacity) provide strong macro tailwinds.
