# Fleetzero

**Sector**: Offshore Energy
**Official Site**: https://www.fleetzero.com/

## Overview
Fleetzero is a marine technology company pioneering the electrification of ocean freight. The company designs, manufactures, and sells ultra energy-dense modular marine battery systems (Leviathan) and hybrid/electric propulsion systems for commercial vessels. Founded 2021 in Houston, TX, Fleetzero's mission is to decarbonize ocean freight without a green premium — achieving price parity with fossil fuel solutions.

## Business Model
- **Direct sales + end-to-end integration**: Custom hybrid/electric propulsion solutions from concept to commissioning
- **Price parity strategy**: No green premium; payback under 6 years, positive within 2 years for many projects
- **Modular approach**: Leviathan batteries scale from smallest support vessels to largest ships; modular hull construction lowers shipbuilding costs
- **Battery barge service**: 55 MWh mobile charging for multiple vessels

## Key Technology
- **Leviathan Modular Energy Storage**: 3.8 MWh per container at 25% standard container footprint; double the power at half the price vs. competing marine batteries
- **Modular Hull Construction**: Pioneering modular hull construction to lower shipyard costs and increase throughput — directly addresses the shipbuilding capacity bottleneck
- **Hybrid Propulsion Systems**: Custom solutions for existing and new vessels, extending vessel life
- **Battery Barge**: 55 MWh expandable mobile charging infrastructure

## Data & Measurement Needs
- **Primary data types**: Vessel power consumption profiles, route energy modeling, battery state-of-charge telemetry, propulsion system performance data
- **Key measurements/parameters**: Energy density (kWh/kg), power output (MW), charge/discharge cycles, thermal management data, hull resistance coefficients, vessel speed-power curves
- **Observation platforms/programs**: Onboard sensor networks for battery health monitoring, propulsion efficiency tracking, voyage energy optimization
- **Known data gaps**: Long-term marine battery degradation data in real ocean conditions; correlation between biofouling and propulsion energy demand; real-world battery performance under extreme sea states
- **Interest in external data services**: Metocean data for voyage optimization and energy budgeting; wave height/current data for power forecasting; port infrastructure data for charging logistics
