# Ebb Carbon

**Official Site**: https://www.ebbcarbon.com/
**Last Updated**: 2026-05-17

**Business Model**: Electrochemical processing of desalination brine — recovers freshwater, produces CDR via ocean alkalinity, and low-cost industrial chemicals.
**Key Tech**: Modular electrochemical membrane stacks.
**Funding/Backers**: Microsoft, Stripe CDR purchases; DOE/PNNL pilots; Isometric verification.
**Recent (2026)**: Google collaboration; Project Macoma learnings published.

## Data & Measurement Needs

- **Primary data types**: Seawater carbonate chemistry (pH, alkalinity, DIC), ecological monitoring data for outfall impact (plankton, shellfish), seawater chemistry at intake and discharge
- **Key measurements/parameters**: pH, total alkalinity, DIC, dissolved oxygen, salinity, temperature, plankton biomass, shellfish health indicators, brine chemistry
- **Observation platforms/programs**: Inline sensors at electrochemical stack, periodic seawater sampling campaigns, ecological baseline surveys at outfall sites, PNNL/DOE pilot monitoring
- **Known data gaps**: Continuous real-time carbonate chemistry sensing at depth; affordable pH sensors for long-term deployment at multiple sites; ecological impact baselines for discharge zones
- **Interest in external data services**: High — requires extensive MRV data for carbon credit verification; would benefit from coastal sensor networks (e.g. Coastal Measures CUMULUS platform)
