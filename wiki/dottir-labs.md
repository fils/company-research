# Dottir Labs

**Sector**: Ocean Data & AI
**Official Site**: https://dottirlabs.com/
**HQ**: Somerville, MA, USA
**Last Updated**: 2026-05-17

## Overview

Dottir Labs is an MIT spinout (2023) developing real-time, molecular-level water quality monitoring systems using patented Raman spectroscopy. The company's sensor technology enables reagent-free, non-destructive chemical analysis of water and process fluids for aquaculture, biotech, oil & gas, and chemical manufacturing.

**Tagline:** "Raman Spectroscopy – Made Accessible"

## Business Model

Hardware + software sensor systems. Dottir Labs sells benchtop and inline Raman spectroscopy sensors with accompanying data analytics. Applications span multiple industries, with aquaculture real-time water quality monitoring as a key growth vertical.

## Key Technology

- **Patented Raman Spectroscopy** — Real-time chemical analysis without reagents or sample destruction
- **Multi-chemical Detection** — Nutrients, metabolic markers, organic waste, inorganic compounds
- **Continuous/Timed Measurement** — Configurable real-time or scheduled sensing
- **Made in USA** — Full manufacturing and quality control in the United States

## Data & Measurement Needs

- **Primary data types**: Water chemistry profiles, Raman spectroscopy spectra, inline process analytics data
- **Key measurements/parameters**: Dissolved nutrients, metabolic markers for aquaculture health, organic/inorganic waste products, chemical composition of process fluids, contextual parameters (pH, temperature, salinity)
- **Observation platforms/programs**: In-tank real-time monitoring for aquaculture, industrial process inline monitoring, field-deployable chemical sensing
- **Known data gaps**: Real-time water chemistry data currently requires lab analysis or reagent-based sensors; aquaculture lacks affordable inline chemical monitoring at molecular level; existing sensors measure physical parameters but not chemical composition in real time
- **Interest in external data services**: Moderate — core technology is proprietary sensor hardware; data analytics platform is complementary

## Relevance to Marine Data Markets

Dottir Labs addresses a critical gap in ocean CDR MRV and aquaculture monitoring: real-time water chemistry data. Current sensor networks measure physical parameters (temperature, DO, salinity) but Dottir's Raman spectroscopy adds molecular-level chemical insight (pH, DIC, alkalinity, nutrient profiles) without reagents. This directly serves the MRV data needs of companies like Ebb Carbon, Planetary Technologies, and other ocean alkalinity enhancement firms, and the water quality monitoring needs of aquaculture operators.
