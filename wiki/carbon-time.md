# Carbon Time

**Sector**: Ocean Carbon Sequestration
**Official Site**: https://www.carbon-time.com/

## Overview
European CDR pioneer specializing in Ocean Alkalinity Enhancement (OAE). Increases ocean capacity to permanently sequester atmospheric CO2 on geological timescales (20,000+ year permanence).

## Business Model
- OAE carbon removal credits
- Multiple deployment approaches: mineral weathering, electrochemical, biological methods, coastal preservation
- Partnerships with marine industries (aquaculture, desalination, dredging)

## Funding & Backing
- **Team for the Planet** (non-profit aiming for €1B climate innovation fund)
- **Corporate partners**: Google, Microsoft, BPI France, La French Tech
- **Academic**: University of Antwerp, French Ministry of Ecological Transition

## Key Technology
Four OAE methods:
1. Natural rock weathering (olivine, carbonates, silicates)
2. Electrochemical (synergy with desalination)
3. Biological (oyster farms, enhanced bioturbation)
4. Coastal preservation (mangroves, seagrass, salt marshes)

## Data & Measurement Needs
- Primary data types: Ocean alkalinity measurements, satellite ocean color, sensor network telemetry, coastal water chemistry
- Key measurements: pH, total alkalinity, DIC, carbonate saturation state, nutrients, CO2 flux
- Observation platforms: Coastal sensor arrays, satellite validation, AUV campaigns, fixed moorings
- Known data gaps: Spatial coverage for dispersal modeling, real-time alkalinity monitoring, long-term impact baselines
- Interest in external data services: Very high — OAE MRV requires comprehensive ocean monitoring infrastructure
