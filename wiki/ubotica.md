# Ubotica

**Sector**: Ocean Data & AI
**Official Site**: https://ubotica.com/

## Overview
Ubotica Technologies is an Irish space technology firm pioneering Orbital AI / Cognitive Earth Observation for real-time maritime intelligence from space. The company's SPACE:AI platform enables satellites to think, see, and act autonomously — processing AI at the edge (in orbit) to detect threats, track suspicious vessels, and deliver decision-grade intelligence in near-real-time. This represents a paradigm shift from traditional Earth observation (observe then think) to Cognitive EO (predict, task, process, learn, deliver).

## Business Model
- **Subscription-based SaaS**: Predictive maritime surveillance subscription for government/defense customers
- **Edge AI licensing**: SPACE:AI platform deployment for satellite constellation operators
- **Partnership revenue**: Joint solutions with Fugro (space-to-seabed), NASA JPL (autonomous satellite networks)

## Funding
- **$11M funding round** (Jun 2026) — to scale real-time maritime intelligence from space

## Key Technology
- **SPACE:AI Platform**: 11 missions successfully flown; 30+ AI models deployed (vessel detection, autonomous targeting, anomaly detection)
- **Cognitive EO Paradigm**: Predicts risk → decides what to task → processes at edge → learns from every cycle → delivers actionable intelligence
- **Edge AI Processing**: AI runs directly on satellites, eliminating ground station download delays for near-real-time intelligence
- **Dark Vessel Detection**: Detects vessels that escape AIS and radar — shadow fleets, illegal fishing, smuggling

## Key Partnerships
- **Fugro**: Space-to-seabed maritime intelligence — combines satellite EO with Fugro's global ocean infrastructure (subsea sensors, offshore vessels, USVs, UAVs)
- **NASA JPL**: FAME (Federated Autonomous MEasurement) — autonomous intelligent satellite network demonstration, with Open Cosmos
- **ESA**: European Space Agency partner
- **NOVI Space**: Real-time AI inference at the space edge
- Additional partners: Thales, GMV, CGI, Deimos Elecnor, Cobham, Frontgrade, Unibap, Simera Sense, Apex Space, City University of London

## Team & Location
- Dublin, Ireland HQ
- Proven with ESA, NASA JPL, and commercial satellite operators

## Data & Measurement Needs
- **Primary data types**: Satellite imagery (optical, SAR, multispectral), AIS data, vessel detection AI model outputs, edge-processed threat assessments, subsea sensor data (via Fugro partnership)
- **Key measurements/parameters**: Vessel positions, vessel types, speed/heading, dark vessel detections, maritime traffic patterns, anomalous behavior indicators, ocean surface conditions (for satellite tasking optimization)
- **Observation platforms/programs**: Satellite constellations with edge AI (11 missions flown), Fugro subsea sensor networks, USV fleets, UAVs — unified space-to-seabed intelligence layer
- **Known data gaps**: Real-time ocean surface conditions for satellite tasking optimization; fusion of satellite-derived vessel data with in-situ oceanographic measurements; persistent monitoring of high-threat maritime corridors
- **Interest in external data services**: Oceanographic data for contextualizing vessel behavior; weather/sea state data for satellite tasking; integration with coastal sensor networks (potential link to Coastal Measures CUMULUS, Quartermaster SmartMast)

## Cross-Sector Connections
- **Ocean Data & AI**: Ubotica extends the sector to space — complementing Amphitrite (satellite data fusion), Sofar Ocean (ocean sensor network), Quartermaster (vessel-mounted sensing), and Coastal Measures (coastal sensor aggregation)
- **Maritime Operations**: Dark vessel detection supports maritime security, naval operations, and fisheries enforcement — connects to Orca AI (vessel awareness), SEA.AI (optical gap filling)
- **Fugro Partnership**: Creates a unique space-to-seabed intelligence stack — satellite EO + subsea sensors + USVs + UAVs, potentially the most comprehensive maritime intelligence layer in the knowledge graph
