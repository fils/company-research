# Sea Machines Robotics

**Sector**: Maritime Operations & Analytics
**Official Site**: https://sea-machines.com/
**Last Updated**: 2026-07-13

**Focus**: Autonomous control and navigation systems for commercial and defense vessels; dual-use USVs and autonomy systems.
**Status**: 200+ deliveries worldwide; record year of bookings 2026; US Navy MASC program participant.
**Founded**: 2015, Boston, MA

## Business Model
Hardware sales (autonomy systems + USVs) + software subscriptions (SM Link APIs, FleetViewer). Dual-use commercial + defense revenue streams. Retrofits existing vessels and integrates with new builds. Export-ready with production facilities in US and Europe. Vessels from 10ft RHIBs to 300ft workboats; diesel, electric, jet, hybrid propulsion.

## Data & Measurement Needs

- **Primary data types**: Real-time vessel sensor streams (GPS, IMU, compass, radar, AIS), computer vision feeds (AI-ris 4K cameras), environment sensors, control telemetry, hydrographic data
- **Key measurements/parameters**: Vessel position, heading, speed, attitude, obstacle detection/classification, sea state, wind, wave height, GPS signal integrity (spoofing/jamming detection)
- **Observation platforms/programs**: Vessel-mounted autonomy systems (SM300), USV missions (SELKIE, STORMRUNNER), fleet-level remote monitoring (FleetViewer), GPS-denied navigation testing
- **Known data gaps**: GPS-denied operations require alternative navigation sources (inertial, visual); limited autonomous navigation in extreme weather; needs high-resolution bathymetric data for shallow water operations
- **Interest in external data services**: Hydrographic charts, real-time weather/oceanographic feeds, satellite communications for beyond-line-of-sight USV control, AIS augmentation
