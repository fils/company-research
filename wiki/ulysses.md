# Ulysses

**Sector**: Marine Monitoring & Sensors
**Official Site**: https://www.theoceancompany.com/

**Overview**
Ulysses (legal entity: The Ocean Company) builds small, low-cost, modular autonomous underwater vehicles (AUVs) and dual-domain surface/subsea systems for marine data acquisition, ecosystem restoration, and defense missions. Founded in Dublin (Ireland); now HQ in San Francisco. Vertically integrated manufacturing. Customers include US Navy, VIMS, Mote Marine Laboratory, Great Barrier Reef Foundation, The Nature Conservancy, and Government of Australia.

**Technology**
- **Mako AUV** — 72hr endurance, 200 lb max payload, 5,000 ft depth rating
- **Kraken** — autonomous launch, recovery, and recharge system (zero human intervention)
- Modular "Lego" design — swappable payloads, batteries, compute, sensors via standardized ports ("like an API")
- Cooperative swarming — vehicles intercommunicate; stealthy low-bandwidth comms
- 40x more subsea computing power than small/medium vehicle competitors (edge AI)
- Advanced navigation in GPS/vision/comms-denied environments

**Business Model**
- Sale/lease of Mako AUV platforms to government, research, and commercial customers
- "As-a-service" autonomous missions for subsea survey, inspection, intervention
- Defense contracts (US Department of War, allied navies)
- Ecosystem restoration services (seagrass planting, reef monitoring)
- Vertically integrated manufacturing in San Francisco

**Recent Highlights**
- $46M total raised: seed + Series A (April 2026)
- Series A led by Andreessen Horowitz American Dynamism fund (Erin Price-Wright, Ryan McEntush)
- Harpoon Ventures, Superorganism, Quiet Capital participating
- AUV unit cost: $50,000 (50x cheaper than legacy providers)

### Data & Measurement Needs
- Primary data types: AUV-mounted acoustic/optical sensor data, real-time cooperative swarm telemetry, bathymetric mapping at 5,000 ft depth, subsea environmental data, marine ecosystem baseline data, subsea infrastructure inspection imagery
- Key measurements/parameters: Subsea temperature, salinity, dissolved oxygen; current velocity profiles; acoustic backscatter (multibeam, side-scan); optical imagery; bathymetry/seafloor topography; marine biomass/seagrass coverage; coral health
- Observation platforms/programs: Mako AUV fleet (swarm-capable), Kraken surface vessel (launch/recovery/recharge), modular sensor payloads, edge AI compute nodes onboard
- Known data gaps: Scalable persistent ocean observation at depth; reliable navigation in GPS/comm-denied undersea environments; real-time swarm-coordinated data relay to shore; high-resolution ecosystem baseline data for restoration targets
- Interest in external data services: High — mission planning, ecosystem baseline establishment, and post-mission analysis all benefit from integrating external oceanographic and remote sensing data
