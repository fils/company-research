# SeaDeep

**Sector**: Ocean Data & AI
**Official Site**: https://www.seadeep.io/

## Overview
SeaDeep develops AI solutions to understand and protect the ocean, empowering marine robotics with autonomy for mapping and inspecting underwater environments. Partners with Seabed 2030 for ocean exploration.

## Business Model
AI platform and advisory services for blue economy stakeholders; grant-funded development with focus on practical deployments for industry and research.

## Funding
Over $1M in grants; early-stage with strong non-dilutive support.

## Key Technology
AI-powered underwater computer vision, autonomous navigation for AUVs, real-time ocean data processing and intelligence extraction.

### Data & Measurement Needs
- **Primary data types:** Underwater imagery/video, acoustic sensor data, AUV telemetry, bathymetric maps
- **Key measurements/parameters:** Seafloor topography, marine life detection metrics, pipeline/cable integrity indicators, water column properties
- **Observation platforms/programs:** AUV/ROV deployments, partnerships with Seabed 2030 mapping initiatives, offshore infrastructure monitoring
- **Known data gaps:** High-resolution real-time AI training datasets for diverse ocean conditions; standardized underwater data formats
- **Interest in external data services:** Public oceanographic datasets, satellite-derived ocean color for validation, NOAA marine data feeds

## Connections
Related to marine monitoring, offshore energy inspection, coral reef protection sectors.
