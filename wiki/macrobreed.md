# MacroBreed

**Sector**: Aquaculture
**Official Site**: https://macrobreed.com/
**Last Updated**: 2026-05-24

## Business Model
MacroBreed pioneers sustainable kelp breeding using cutting-edge genomic solutions to increase kelp yield and enable expansion of safe ocean farming zones. Their patented non-reproductive kelp strain addresses regulatory barriers for kelp farming expansion.

## Business Model
- **Kelp genomics**: Genomic selection breeding programs for improved kelp strains
- **Non-reproductive cultivars**: Patented sporeless kelp breeds enabling safer commercial aquaculture
- **Elite cultivars**: High-yield, resilient kelp varieties for commercial farmers
- **Seed banks**: Diverse kelp genetic repositories for breeding programs

## Funding
- **$11M+** in competitive grants (ARPA-E, USDA SBIR)
- **VentureWell/NOAA OEA Stage 0** (Spring 2026) — Pre-commercialization workshop
- Activate fellowship
- Partners: UConn, WHOI, UWM

## Key Technology
- Genome Assembly & Annotation: Reference genomes for *Macrocystis pyrifera* (giant kelp) and *Saccharina latissima* (sugar kelp) on JGI PhycoCosm
- Patented non-reproductive kelp strain (PCT/US2024/027903)
- **300% increase** in kelp yield from genomic selection breeding trials
- Seed banks and elite cultivar development

## Data & Measurement Needs
- **Primary data types**: Genomic sequencing data, kelp phenotype measurements, oceanographic baseline data (temp, nutrients), aquaculture yield data, ecosystem impact assessments
- **Key measurements/parameters**: Genetic markers, growth rates, biomass yield, nutrient uptake rates, water temperature, dissolved nutrients (nitrate, phosphate), salinity, light availability
- **Observation platforms/programs**: Kelp farm monitoring, oceanographic sensor networks, genomic sequencing laboratories, field trial deployments
- **Known data gaps**: Limited genomic baseline data for wild kelp populations; sparse long-term ecosystem impact data for cultivated kelp; need for standardized phenotyping protocols across sites
- **Interest in external data services**: NOAA oceanographic monitoring data, satellite sea surface temperature/chlorophyll, regional nutrient cycling models, aquaculture environmental permitting data
